## Current design weakness relative to Wolfram fidelity target

The poster still contains a central numbered box-stack (`Step 1` ... `Step 10`) that behaves like a conventional infographic. This conflicts with the stronger design principle established in the conversation:

\[
\boxed{\text{the mathematics itself should become the visual structure.}}
\]

Therefore future visual work should treat the current frame as an editable intermediate, not as a final fidelity target.

## Binding visual grammar

Reference: Wolfram Physics Project Visual Summary and the Stephen Wolfram 2020 article supplied by the user.

Use:
- causal/multiway graphs;
- hypergraphs;
- state-transition structures;
- trajectories/vector fields;
- state-space and viability boundaries;
- uncertainty geometry;
- branchial/alternative decision structures;
- sparse labels and mathematically meaningful line/edge semantics;
- editable vectors/graph primitives.

Avoid:
- card-heavy layouts;
- generic icons as the main explanatory mechanism;
- decorative networks with no mathematical semantics;
- repeated boxes whose text merely describes mathematics;
- arrows without relation meaning.
