## How to use the candidates without scope inflation

A future chat may borrow a **method** from another candidate while keeping the frozen MSE object unchanged. Examples:

- Candidate II supplies an inference/UQ module for \(Z_{ij}\) without changing the primary research question.
- Candidate IV supplies stochastic/hybrid dynamics for modes \(m(t)\).
- Candidate VI supplies subgroup service/deprivation constraints inside \(K_R(t)\).
- Candidate V supplies minimum enhancement/resource allocation after \(B_R\) is defined.
- Candidate VII supplies a surrogate only if it preserves the relevant recovery/viability classification within declared tolerance.

A future chat must label any attempt to make one of the seven candidates the new thesis identity as `PROPOSED_SCOPE_CHANGE` until the user explicitly freezes it.
