# Scope, Authenticity, and Limits

## What this archive preserves

This archive is designed for **high-rigor recall and reproducibility**, not for narrative convenience. It preserves:
- the scientific architecture discussed in the conversation;
- equations and candidate formulations;
- the seven thesis candidates;
- the six scientific chains and their layers;
- the Operations Research + Monte Carlo decision model and exact generated outputs available in the session;
- the mathematical knowledge-graph / ontology / evidence-level redesign;
- the Wolfram Physics Project visual-design reverse-engineering and Figma reconstruction decisions;
- persistent source URLs and DOI identifiers used in the conversation;
- exact pasted Markdown attachments available in the current conversation;
- hashes/inventory of generated and uploaded files that were available in the session.

## What this archive does not claim

1. It is **not a verbatim export of every ChatGPT message**. The system available to this archive does not expose a full raw transcript export for every skipped/condensed turn.
2. It does **not preserve hidden chain-of-thought** or private model scratch work.
3. It does **not invent missing prompts or assistant responses**.
4. Raster images and the 25 MB course ZIP are inventoried by filename, file ID (when available), size and SHA-256; they are not embedded into this text-only GitHub preservation commit.
5. Literature claims recorded from prior responses must be re-verified against primary sources before publication if the original source is not itself stored in the repository.

## Authenticity classes used here

- `VERBATIM_ATTACHMENT`: copied byte-for-byte from a current-conversation text attachment.
- `GENERATED_ARTIFACT`: exact code/result file generated during the conversation.
- `RECONSTRUCTED_SUMMARY`: faithful synthesis of visible conversation state; not a verbatim transcript.
- `EXTERNAL_SOURCE`: URL/DOI cited in the conversation.
- `MODEL_ASSUMPTION`: modelling choice used for Monte Carlo/OR or framework construction.
- `RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS`: proposed gap, proposition, or relation requiring falsification.

## Duplicate preservation

Two 2026-08-20 pasted Markdown files have identical SHA-256 hashes. Both filenames are retained in the manifest because filename/history is part of provenance, even though their content is duplicate.
