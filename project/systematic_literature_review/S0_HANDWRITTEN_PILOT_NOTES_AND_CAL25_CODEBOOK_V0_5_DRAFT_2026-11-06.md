## Operational choices in the **proposed v0.5 unsigned draft**

1. At title/abstract phase, independent human reviewer records only **INCLUDE / EXCLUDE / UNCLEAR**, evidence and appropriate source-specific reason; this is an *advance to full-text screening* decision.
2. Evidence role separately codes **CORE_CANDIDATE**, SUPPORTING_PHYSICS, SUPPORTING_METHOD, SUPPORTING_SERVICE, BACKGROUND_CONTEXT or NOT_APPLICABLE.
3. A quantitative **one-way EV charging → distribution transformer thermal/loss-of-life** pathway may qualify as a CORE_CANDIDATE even though actual bidirectional P↔T causal observation is not shown. An observed grid-to-charger restriction requires separate chronology/commands/actual charging-service evidence.
4. Distinguish harmonic THD, reactive power, line losses, modeled ageing, true temperature measurements, charging cost, delivered energy and mobility mission. These are not interchangeable evidence.
5. Context-only reviews/standards/proceedings are supporting *background*, not independent measured studies. Context contribution is excluded from the proposed random100 core+supporting precision numerator. Final evidence-role policy still requires human signoff.
6. Preserve source-authentic I1–I6, E01–E07; unresolved evidence ambiguity becomes UNCLEAR rather than unjustified exclusion.
