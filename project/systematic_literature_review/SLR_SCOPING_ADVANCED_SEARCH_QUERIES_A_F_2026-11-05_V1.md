# Systematic Scoping Review — Advanced Search Queries A–F

| Scope | Search objective |
|---|---|
| A | Transformer thermal dynamics and EV charging |
| B | EVSE thermal management and extreme-heat derating |
| C | Measured connected transformer–EVSE installations |
| D | Coupled dynamics and electricity/mobility resilience |
| E | Physics-informed thermal dynamics and parameter identification |
| F | Engineering standards and physics-model validation |

## Scopus — Advanced Search

### A

```text
TITLE-ABS-KEY(("distribution transformer" OR "power transformer") AND ("electric vehicle" OR "EV charging" OR "charging load") AND (thermal OR temperature OR hotspot OR "hot spot" OR "top oil" OR "dynamic thermal rating"))
```

### B

```text
TITLE-ABS-KEY(("fast charger" OR "DC charger" OR EVSE OR rectifier) AND ("ambient temperature" OR "high temperature" OR "extreme heat" OR heatwave OR cooling) AND (derating OR "power limit" OR "offered power" OR efficiency))
```

### C

```text
TITLE-ABS-KEY(("charging station" OR "electric bus depot" OR EVSE) AND (transformer OR feeder OR substation) AND (measur* OR monitoring OR experiment* OR metered))
```

### D

```text
TITLE-ABS-KEY(("power transportation" OR "power-transportation" OR "coupled power and transportation" OR "charging network") AND (resilience OR recovery OR cascading OR delay OR service OR thermal))
```

### E

```text
TITLE-ABS-KEY(("physics-informed" OR PINN OR "physics-informed neural network" OR "hybrid physics" OR "neural operator") AND (transformer OR "EV battery" OR charger OR "battery thermal") AND (temperature OR thermal OR "heat equation" OR "parameter identification"))
```

### F

```text
TITLE-ABS-KEY(("IEC 60076-7" OR "IEEE C57.91" OR "transformer loading guide" OR "dynamic thermal model" OR "battery thermal model") AND (validation OR measurement OR cooling OR loss OR temperature))
```
