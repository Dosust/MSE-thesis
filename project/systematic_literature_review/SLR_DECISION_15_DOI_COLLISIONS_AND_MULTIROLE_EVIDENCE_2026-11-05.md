# SLR-DEC-2026-11-05-DOI-01 — shared-DOI identity and multi-role evidence

**Date:** 2026-11-05  
**Decision authority:** User-approved research protocol decision  
**Status:** Multi-search/multi-role policy APPROVED; 15 DOI-pair identity verifications PENDING.  
**Thesis:** Engineering of Power–Electrified Transportation Interdependencies for Resilience.

## Finding

The 12 Scopus export files A0–F1 contain 29,736 retrieval occurrences and 23,862 unique EID-level records after removing 5,874 exact repeated EID occurrences across exports. **15 DOI values each appear under two distinct Scopus EIDs** (30 affected EID records). These are potential identity collisions, **not yet 15 proven duplicate works**. Separately, 104 title/year groups require manual identity review. Some DOI collisions occur within one search block, not only across searches.

## Research decision, reasons and safeguards

1. **Retain all search provenance (A0–F1).** Preserve unchanged originals, every EID, DOI, source block, search-row provenance and original metadata. Why: independently repeatable searches can retrieve the same record for different reasons; overwriting erases reproducibility.
2. **Do not automatically collapse or delete the 15 DOI pairs.** Link each pair as a provisional DOI family. Compare publisher/Crossref DOI metadata with Scopus titles, authors, year, publication type, publication venue, pages and full text. Record reviewer, review date and evidence link. Why: two EIDs with one DOI may reflect record-version or metadata problems; DOI equality alone does not prove two independent records are one work.
3. **One verified work is counted once, but may have multiple scientific roles.** When same-work identity is positively verified, assign a canonical work ID with both EIDs as aliases and preserve all source searches. Allow distinct, evidence-grounded SO1 (physical interdependencies), SO2 (electrothermal equations/validation) and SO3 (timing, resilience, charging/mobility service) contribution tags, plus supporting/standards/method/contrary-evidence roles. **A role is not another publication or study.** Why: enables multidisciplinary synthesis without double-counting.
4. **If identity differs or remains unclear, preserve the distinct records/uncertainty** with an auditable explanation; do not silently subtract cases from 23,862. Two publications with distinct DOIs may also arise from one empirical study family and require separate study-family reconciliation.
5. **Screening is independent of retrieval count or role count.** Assign scientific roles only after checking actual relevant evidence; do not infer inclusion from title keywords or Scopus search block. Human reviewers must validate the 25-paper pilot and final eligibility. Report EID-level, bibliographic-work-level, and independent-study-level counts separately.
