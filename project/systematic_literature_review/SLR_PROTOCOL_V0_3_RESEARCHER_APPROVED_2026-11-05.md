## 2. Research scope and questions (existing canonical authority retained)
Focus: thermally conditioned, state-time dependent interaction between the Power System and Electrified Transportation System under extreme heat. Do not expand scope to other systems or replace the official objectives.

- **SO1:** Identify and qualify thermally sensitive physical states and directional thermal interdependencies connecting power and electrified transportation.
- **SO2:** Develop a physics-based state-time model of the qualified mechanisms and propagation under extreme heat.
- **SO3:** Quantify critical transition times, interdependency propagation delays and resulting electricity and mobility service consequences.

The scoping review will address: physical P→T and T→P pathways; original governing heat/electrical laws and validation; measured versus modeled states and connectivity; thermal derating and service outcomes; closest prior art and disconfirming evidence.

**PCC:** Population/participants = distribution transformers, feeders, charging stations, EVSE, EVs/batteries, depots; Concept = thermal mechanisms, constraints, measured state-time effects, governing models, resilience consequences; Context = extreme ambient heat is primary hazard, with other-temperature physics comparators distinctly coded.

## 3. Researcher-approved eligibility framework

### Include
- **I1 Time:** Indexed primary searches cover publication years 2000–2026 through the actual search date; earlier foundational references can enter as separately traced citation-chaining context.
- **I2 Language:** All languages at search stage; translation ability and final restrictions logged.
- **I3 Systems:** Distribution grids, transformer/feeder, EV charging infrastructure, EV batteries and physically relevant thermal components.
- **I4 Physics/contribution:** Meaningful connection to SO1–SO3 or technically justified transferable heat-transfer, electrical loss, temperature/rating, parameter, measurement or service behavior.
- **I5 Study type:** Analytical, simulation, numerical, laboratory, observational, field, original technical studies; classify source and evidence design explicitly.
- **I6 Temperature:** Extreme heat is primary; ordinary-temperature measured/physics comparators may be included for transferability with no false high-heat claim.

### Exclude or reclassify (with auditable decisions and provenance)
- **E01** wrong physical system without defensible transfer.
- **E02** no relevant thermal, physics, interface, method or service evidence.
- **E03** unrelated economics, cyber or generic AI without grounded physical/service contribution.
- **E04** secondary material misrepresented as original measurement: *reclassify* context/standard when relevant.
- **E05** non-assessable source after logged retrieval attempts; prefer UNCLEAR before full-text assessment.
- **E06** outside approved indexed-search date scope; log foundational exception.
- **E07** confirmed duplicate **work** after identity adjudication, preserving all EIDs, DOIs and search-occurrence links.

**Evidence roles:** CORE = directly qualified physical heat-dependent P↔T coupling; SUPPORTING PHYSICS = transferrable transformer/EVSE/battery thermal law; SUPPORTING METHOD = physical/numerical/parameter identification/validation methodology; SUPPORTING SERVICE = demand, capacity, delivered charging or mobility performance; BACKGROUND = review/standard/methodological background, not independent field validation. One publication may fulfill several SO roles if evidence supports each role.

**Resolve and document before formal screening:** (i) minimum direct-physical connectivity for CORE; (ii) service-only context placement in flow denominator; (iii) standards/preprints/conference reviews/retracted study policies; (iv) published-work vs independent-study identity handling; (v) inaccessible full texts and translation exclusions; (vi) which exact full-text verification gates determine an evidence claim.
