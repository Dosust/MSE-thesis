## 1. Formal resilience remains primary

~~~math
\boxed{
\mathfrak R_T
\neq
\mathcal D_R.
}
~~~

\(\mathfrak R_T\) remains the formal remain/return/confidence carrier.

\(\mathcal D_R\) remains the supporting diagnosis object.

## 2. Permitted data-driven diagnostics

Subject to validation and provenance, \(\mathcal D_R\) may contain:
- recovery-duration features;
- spatial performance maps;
- PCA scores/loadings;
- cluster/archetype labels;
- feature contributions;
- group-stratified summaries;
- post-event diagnostic classifications.

No diagnostic becomes the formal resilience definition unless a future authority explicitly changes Stage 5.

## 3. Reference-state requirement

Recovery or degradation features must identify their comparison object.

~~~math
\boxed{
\text{observed recovery metric}
=
f(\text{event trajectory},\text{reference trajectory}).
}
~~~

The reference is versioned and cannot be chosen after seeing confirmatory results.

## 4. Diagnostic-versus-causal firewall

~~~math
\boxed{
\text{diagnostic association}
\neq
\text{causal interface importance}
\neq
C_e^{\mathcal V}.
}
~~~

A spatial cluster cannot establish a causal mechanism or an intervention effect.

## 5. Spatial-resolution rule

Where aggregation changes engineering interpretation, Stage 5 retains spatially resolved QOIs/diagnostics before aggregate summaries.

## 6. H3 firewall

Data-driven diagnostics may complement H3 interpretation but do not establish that viability/reachability adds incremental value.

~~~math
\boxed{
\mathcal D_R\ \text{informative}
\not\Rightarrow
H3\ \text{SUPPORTED}.
}
~~~
