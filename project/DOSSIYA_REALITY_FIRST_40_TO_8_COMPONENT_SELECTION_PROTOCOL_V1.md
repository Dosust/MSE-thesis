## 2. The physical reality to observe BEFORE selection

Fixed thesis-wide systems: Power System P and complete Electrified Transportation System T_e under external extreme heat H(x,t). A transformer–feeder–EVSE–vehicle installation is an **illustrative bounded case**, not a replacement definition for either system. First establish:
- **Physical case:** what exact transformer (oil or dry), feeder, charger topology (conductive DC versus IPT etc.), vehicle, location and duty cycle exist together? Nameplate, connectivity, ratings, devices and measurement boundaries. UNKNOWN is allowed.
- **Heat:** ambient temperature, solar radiation, wind/airflow and material/contact/cooling environment as justified; spatial and temporal exposure, not automatically a coupled mechanism.
- **Electrical paths:** actual AC supply, phase currents, power factor/reactive load and grid-side EVSE P_AC; distinguish conversion losses, DC output, battery acceptance and battery-terminal energy.
- **Heat/controls:** internal heat generation/storage/rejection, temperature-location/sensor, permissible thermal envelope, manufacturer-controlled derating, actual relay/dispatch behavior and recovery.
- **State/time/service:** temperature or energy storage vs algebraic readings; thermal transitions vs causal propagation vs service impact vs recovery; observed delivered electricity vs required electricity and battery departure-readiness proxy vs observed vehicle mobility.
- **Physical rivals:** common heat exposure, non-EV loads, SOC and vehicle-request limits, control schedules, upstream shortages, cooling actions and measurement error.

**No directional thermal effect is established by a general P<->T_e diagram.** Test T_e→P: EVSE temperature/control changes its *actual AC demand*, changing transformer loading/thermal history. Test P→T_e: transformer thermal state activates a verified operational loading restriction that actually changes EVSE permitted/received supply. Ordinary AC electricity supply is not itself proof of heat-conditioned P→T_e influence.

## 4. Noncompensatory component admission gates

Apply to every enumerated candidate in order. A score can NEVER compensate for a failed hard gate.

| Gate | Decision question | What passes | What fails/remains OPEN |
|---|---|---|---|
| G0 Inventory | Is this a uniquely defined physical candidate in the specified P/T_e system, traceable to the forty source entries? | ID, actual object, hierarchy and system membership | Duplicate, unknown source row, interface-only quantity mistaken for equipment |
| G1 Reality | Is this equipment present or defensibly specified in a mutually compatible bounded demonstrator? | Device/configuration/rating and actual interfaces documented | Mixed incompatible equipment, imaginary installation, irrelevant component |
| G2 Thermal mechanism | Does extreme heat or self-heating demonstrably alter a relevant state, capability, or well-defined limiting function? | Source-identified physics + thermal quantity + comparison | Ambient correlation alone, no relevant heat mechanism |
| G3 Cross-system/service necessity | Can the component be traced via a justified physical/control path to a directional P/T_e effect OR is it indispensable to represent the required electricity/mobility service? | Source→AC/operational exchange→receiver→state/capability/service with clear role | Generic association, effect outside SO1–SO3, unsupported claimed bidirectionality |
| G4 Equation/evidence/data | Are governing conservation/constitutive/control laws, device parameters, relevant observations and testable validity boundaries available or realistically obtainable? | Exact source type + variables/units + planned calibration/validation or qualified scenario-only status | Unattributed limits, inaccessible indispensable parameters, no testable measurement link |
| G5 Minimum sufficient | Does it add distinct necessary explanatory power to the 8-component portfolio over an algebraic/boundary or aggregated representation? | Documented exclusion/ablation comparison, nonredundant scale, bounded complexity | Decorative completeness, nested subcomponent counted twice, unjustified 40-ODE ambition |

Classify each cell PASS / CONDITIONAL / FAIL / OPEN, with source, limitation, current data and next test. For main eight, require PASS on G0/G1/G2/G3/G5 and G4 with at least a reproducible, source-qualified case; independent empirical validation is separately recorded as NOT_RUN/OPEN until completed. If only conditional data support simulation, label results source-parameterized SCENARIO, not validated real installation. Actual numerical limits and exact coupled constitutive laws remain case-specific OPEN until independently admitted.

## 5. Comparison only AMONG gate-eligible candidates

Compare on:
- E: Strength and specificity of thermal-mechanism evidence.
- I: Contribution to distinguishable P→T_e or T_e→P interaction at measured AC/control boundary.
- T: Material time evolution, thermal inertia, crossing or recovery.
- S: Importance to actual required electricity/mobility services.
- D: Sensor/parameter availability and identifiability for calibration/validation.
- N: Unique information gain versus simpler or already-selected components.
- F: Feasibility within master's thesis scope, code and time.

Use **transparent ordinal profiles**, not invented evidence-based numeric weights or final 100-point scores. Pick a *portfolio* of four per system, ensuring actual port connectivity and no loss of essential mechanism. Under otherwise equal evidence, prefer the smaller physically sufficient model. Rank trade-offs openly and test replacement components. NASA Systems Engineering Handbook explains documenting source data, selection rule, verification/validation and trade-study uncertainties (https://www.nasa.gov/reference/system-engineering-handbook-appendix/); this exact gate is a **THESIS-PROPOSED adaptation, not a NASA standard**.

Illustrative component types ONLY, NOT ACCEPTED 8:
P: transformer; selected feeder conductor/cable; physical breaker/switchgear; installed thermal relay/cooling device if independently needed.
T_e: EVSE power converter; physical EVSE cooling device where independently needed; charging connector/cable; battery pack with documented BMS acceptance behavior.
These may be nested or technologically incompatible depending on the case. A manufacturer EVSE and its cooling subassembly must not be double-counted merely to reach four. A separate BMS control function need not be counted as a distinct physical component; use operating logic if appropriate. ## 6. Paper-to-gate learning — no indiscriminate model transfer

- **Hilshey et al. (2013)**, DOI 10.1109/TSG.2012.2217385: modeled transformer temperature/aging with EV loads and ambient heat; DOES NOT certify a site's implemented transformer→EVSE restriction. Supports G2/G4 for a qualified oil transformer thermal model, and warns that aging ≠ observed outage.
- **Pearson et al. (2026)**, DOI 10.1016/j.applthermaleng.2026.132769: measured/CFD electromagnetic–thermal coupling in IPT pads, supports G2/G4 for relevant inductive equipment ONLY, not conventional DCFC without proof.
- **Nabilaa et al. (2026)**, arXiv:2605.06756: physical thermal-energy model and surrogate/active-learning validation, supports G4/G5 information needs and reduced-model comparisons, NOT charger empirical calibration.
- **Sallem et al. (2025)**, arXiv:2512.20130: dual interactions and experimentally observed critical behavior in cryogenic superconducting networks; G3 conceptual lesson connectivity ≠ thermal dependency, NO transfer of phase-transition exponents or temperatures.
- **Katrašnik et al. (2021)**, DOI 10.1016/j.enconman.2021.114036: detailed battery electrothermal physics; reserve G2/G5 battery-scale escalation for demonstrated relevance and validated battery chemistry.
- **Climate motivation:** IPCC AR6 WGIII reports for 2019 approximately 34% of total net anthropogenic GHG emissions from *energy supply* and 15% from *transport* (https://sdgs.un.org/sites/default/files/2023-01/IPCC_AR6_WGIII_FullReport.pdf, SPM B.2.1). Sector shares are not attributable to the selected transformer or EVSE and cannot establish local emission outcomes. Avoid double-counting indirect power emissions.

For any source, record primary source/page, exact equipment, observed/modelled result, equations and validity domain, actual data accessibility, case transferability, rival explanations, and which gate changes. Literature relevance by title alone cannot pass G4.

## 7. Minimum scientific artifacts before selecting eight

A. **40-row reconciliation ledger**: exact source entry; physical/function/limit/boundary/service type; membership and hierarchy; candidate unique ID.
B. **40-row G0–G5 matrix**: source, physics, evidence, data, accepted/conditional/rejected and reasons.
C. **Four-plus-four portfolio**: eight only after passing gates, each with input → physical transformation → output → thermal susceptibility → directional/service role → time → observations → admitted equation vs OPEN.
D. **Paired interface contract**: actual AC grid-side demand and transformer loading, documented thermal-state→actual control action, separate from shared extreme heat.
E. **Ablation/alternatives test**: show what essential state–time/service inference is lost with each component excluded or replaced; do not interpret a purely simulated difference as field validation.
F. **Reproducible model/test plan**: historical M0/M1/M2 comparator semantics, equipment-specific constraints, conservation, solver check, independent measurement plan, uncertainty and censoring of nonexistent events.

No source-verified eight-component portfolio, valid numerical limits, model-derived results, or independent empirical validation is created merely by issuing this protocol.
