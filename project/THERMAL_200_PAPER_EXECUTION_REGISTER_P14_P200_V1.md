## Interpretation and exclusion rules

- **DOI linked** = a first-pass research *candidate* identified in publisher or scholarly-index search; not a full bibliographic/correction/retraction clearance, and **not** a validated or transferable parameter. Each needs authoritative full text and independent provenance check.
- **UNFILLED** = no verified unique study assigned; do not insert invented titles or equations.
- Studies outside the exact active thermal coupling (e.g. stochastic weather or nonthermal network recovery) are **method/context candidates only**, not direct evidence of a thermal interdependency. A review is background, not a focal experiment.
- Per-paper admission gates: identity and publication status; authors/year/venue/DOI; full text; equations, assumptions, IC/BC and parameter units; physical case and sensor geometry; error denominators and test split; verification/validation; uncertainty; transferability; thermal-state/capability/service chain.
- **No final Power thermal model is frozen.** The 200-paper number is a literature-coverage target, **not** a prerequisite sufficient to validate a model. P01–P13 have prior working audits of unequal evidence depth, not thirteen homogeneous full-text audits.
- Indexed metadata sources give only bibliographic/abstract scope unless first-party detailed text is separately checked. No DOI record here supplies a normative temperature limit or allowable electric capability.
