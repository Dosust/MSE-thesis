# Phase 0 — 0C Closure Memo

**Decision:** `CLOSE 0C — BOUNDED REPRESENTATION-FAMILY SATURATION`  
**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Authority:** Theoretical Decision Gate v1.0  
**Scope:** representation-family search only; not a novelty determination.

## 1. Closure basis

The preregistered saturation rule required continued search whenever a targeted round introduced a new first-class representation family.

Rounds 1–3 changed the taxonomy by adding, among others:

```text
shared-state SoS models;
federated/HLA architectures;
physical-functional service ontologies;
uncertainty-aware semantic models;
knowledge graphs;
HLA/FMI/HELICS interoperability;
SysML/ontology/executable bridges;
compositional open-systems / applied-category-theory formalisms.
```

Round 4 targeted the strongest final challenger — compositional open-system mathematics — and found refinements of that family, but no new first-class representation family.

Therefore the representation-family taxonomy is sufficiently stable for controlled comparison.

## 2. What 0C does establish

0C establishes, within the bounded search:

```text
1. Multilayer structure is prior art.
2. Physical + informational/control + organizational modeling is prior art.
3. Dynamic/time-aware interdependency modeling is prior art.
4. Shared-state SoS representations including decisions/resources/organization are prior art.
5. Semantic/ontology interoperability is prior art.
6. Executable co-simulation interoperability is prior art.
7. Relation-as-object plus attachable metadata is prior art.
8. Syntax/semantics separation and compositional open systems are prior art.
9. Behavioral/safety contracts over compositional system architectures are prior art.
```

These findings constrain the thesis contribution.

## 3. What 0C does NOT establish

0C does not establish:

```text
- universal absence of a dominating formalism;
- novelty of the Phase-0 proposal;
- superiority of M1 over M0;
- necessity of every proposed dependency field;
- empirical validity of any infrastructure dependency;
- a reason to modify the frozen seven-stage framework.
```

Absence claims remain prohibited outside the bounded search statement.
