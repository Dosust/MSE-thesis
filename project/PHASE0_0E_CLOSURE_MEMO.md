```math

```


Both representations pass all five non-compensable gates `G1–G5` on V1–V5.

as native type systems.

## 6. Scientific interpretation

The 0D mapping result showed:

```text
M0 compresses domain affiliation of I/O entities into auxiliary semantic context;
M1 places that affiliation directly in a domain × aspect coordinate.
```

0E now shows that, when the common actor/subsystem evidence is available as preregistered, the compressed information in `M_0` is recoverable without ambiguity in V2 and V3.

Therefore the candidate ontology currently demonstrates:

```text
native explicitness,
not a necessary new engineering capability.
```

Likewise, V5 shows that both ontologies admit a conservative Water extension without changing the relation payload or old P–T semantics.
