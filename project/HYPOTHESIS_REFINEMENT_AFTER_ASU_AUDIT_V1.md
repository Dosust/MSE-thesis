## 1. Reason for amendment

The ASU audit and public novelty-falsification search identify prior art that is broader and closer than a generic novelty narrative would allow. Therefore the thesis contribution must be narrowed before any mathematical framework is presented as novel.

## 2. Claims explicitly removed from novelty space

The thesis shall not claim novelty for any of the following in isolation:

- modeling interdependent infrastructures;
- dynamic infrastructure interdependency;
- cascading-failure propagation;
- synthetic interdependent networks;
- power-water or power-communication coupling;
- EV-grid optimization;
- including governance/information conceptually;
- safe-to-fail resilience;
- SETS framing;
- stochastic/probabilistic viability;
- distance to a viability-kernel boundary;
- generic sensitivity analysis of viability kernels;
- dynamic infrastructure criticality in the organizational sense.

## 3. Refined candidate contribution objects

### C1 — evidence-grounded mechanistic interface composition

```math
\boxed{
\mathfrak I_{ij}^{\alpha\beta}
=
(E_i^\alpha,E_j^\beta,M_{ij},w_{ij},\delta_{ij},\tau_{ij},a_{ij},m,\mathcal H_t)
}
```

Candidate contribution is not the tuple alone; it is the demonstration that its fields can be evidence-qualified, mapped into constituent dynamics, and falsifiably compared against weaker coupling representations.

### C2 — observation/posterior uncertainty into sustainable viability

```math
\boxed{
D_{0:t}
\rightarrow
p(Y_t\mid D_{0:t})
\rightarrow
K_{\rm sus}(t)
\rightarrow
\mathcal V_{\rm sus}(t)
}
```

Candidate contribution is the infrastructure-specific end-to-end propagation from heterogeneous observation uncertainty to service-constrained viability, not stochastic viability itself.

### C3 — interface-parameter sensitivity of a viability margin

```math
\boxed{
\rho_g(Y)=d_{g_\psi}(Y,\partial\mathcal V_{\rm sus}),
\qquad
S_{ij}^{\mathcal V}=-\partial_{w_{ij}}\rho_g(Y)
}
```

Candidate contribution is the use of mechanistically defined interface parameters for ranking, with physical perturbation/intervention validation and matched simpler baselines. Distance-to-boundary alone is not novel.

```math

\quad\text{vs}\quad
M_1\;(\text{static})
\quad\text{vs}\quad

```

The contribution is conditional on measured differences and validation, not on claiming dynamic interdependency is new.

```math

```

because information and organizational relevance are already established in prior ASU/global work.

H3 must formalize and validate service/sustainability constraints and controlled viability. It cannot claim novelty for resilience+sustainability or viability theory alone.

### H4
H4 must explicitly distinguish viability-sensitive interface ranking from ASU's organizational “dynamic criticality” and from pre-existing geometric robustness/distance-to-boundary concepts.

### H5
H5 must be a matched intervention test under equal resource/budget conditions, not a generic optimization exercise.
