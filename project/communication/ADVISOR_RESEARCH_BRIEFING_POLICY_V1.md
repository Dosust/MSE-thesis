# Advisor research briefing policy V1

**Created:** 2026-11-12  
**Scope:** All thesis progress updates, especially co-chair Prof. Pat Phelan.  
**Authority:** Communications rule only. It never changes frozen research title, objectives, evidence gates or model approvals.

## Why this policy exists
Protect the reader's time. Communicate the minimum evidence necessary for an informed scientific decision. Do not equate substantial repository activity with mature research.

## Six-slide default (five to seven permitted)
1. **Question and decision requested:** one-sentence research scope and what feedback is required.
2. **Review status:** what was searched, inspected, extracted and independently checked; give the *denominator* and exact stage for every count.
3. **What prior work establishes:** 4-6 papers, mechanism, equation or measurement, evidence ceiling, and a gap/counterexample.
4. **Analyses conducted:** 2-4 critical results with data source, baseline/control, units, and limits.
5. **Current blocking evidence:** no more than 3 scientific gates; distinguish physical model candidate from empirical finding.
6. **Next actions + advisor decisions:** 3 actions, owners, expected evidence, requested feedback date.

## Selection and verification rules
- One slide = one decision question; one headline = one defensible conclusion.
- Show only **Done**, **Provisional**, **Blocked**, or **Planned**; never turn proposed into completed.
- Mark the distinction between (a) a search hit, (b) deduplicated record, (c) AI-priority label, (d) independent title/abstract decision, (e) eligible full text, (f) extracted original, and (g) experimentally validated result.
- Do not add counts from separate or overlapping bibliographies. Reconcile DOI, EID and study-family identifiers first.
- Every quantitative result must include source, unit, comparator, observation regime, and its major limitation.
- Distinguish measured thermal states from estimated hotspot, numerical reference, controller simulation, laboratory outcome, and coupled same-site field evidence.
- No novelty, bidirectional causal coupling, field viability, PINN superiority, or generalization claim without appropriate evidence.
- Record the strongest known **counterexample** when stating a research gap.
- Put 3-5 clickable repository/source links in deck footer or notes, rather than long references on the slide.
- Exclude broad historical frameworks, full code inventories, raw bibliographic lists, decorative equations and all unrequested workstreams unless they change the current decision.
- The master report may be detailed, but the advisor packet must be skimmable in **5-7 minutes**, with 6 slides normally and a one-paragraph email.

## Review-and-send checklist
- [ ] Check current canonical objective and scope on main.
- [ ] Compare most recent relevant draft PRs and resolve superseded access/status claims.
- [ ] Define last confirmed checkpoint and next evidence gate precisely.
- [ ] Confirm no unapproved PR is presented as merged/frozen.
- [ ] Check all numbers, dates, data units, plot axes and source references.
- [ ] Limit the email to purpose, top 2 findings, top 2 blockers and 2-3 questions.
- [ ] Save timestamped advisor deck plus brief in a separate branch/PR for audit.
- [ ] Request feedback with a reasonable reading interval; do not assert that an email was sent unless the mail system confirms sending.
- [ ] Preserve correspondence feedback as dated decisions, separating requests from formal scientific approval.

**Trigger phrase for future use:** "Apply Advisor Research Briefing Policy V1 and produce a Professor Phelan update from current evidence."
