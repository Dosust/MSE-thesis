# MSE Thesis Scope

## Working title

**Mathematical Physics of Sustainable Infrastructure Resilience**

Current precise formulation:

*Equity-Constrained Viability of Coupled Power–Water–Transportation–Solid-Waste Systems with Dynamic Interfaces under Climate Stress*

## Physical system

- Power (P)
- Water/drainage (W)
- Transportation (T)
- Solid waste (SW)

Primary demonstrator: urban flooding and the endogenous flood–transport–waste–drainage feedback.

## Governing research architecture

`D_obs → (G_hat, theta_hat, Sigma_theta) → F_dyn → K_sus,eq → V_sus,eq → C_sus,eq`

The core scientific question is how accurately dynamic cross-infrastructure interfaces must be identified and represented before sustainable/equitable viability and recovery predictions can be trusted.

## Evidence strategy

The thesis uses two linked tracks:

1. PRISMA / evidence falsification and precedent search.
2. Mathematical development: inverse/identification, dynamic coupling, timescale regimes, viability/HJ reachability, recovery/capture geometry, sustainability/equity constraints, and uncertainty.

Evidence has authority over the starting architecture: a direct precedent is a successful falsification result, not a failure.
