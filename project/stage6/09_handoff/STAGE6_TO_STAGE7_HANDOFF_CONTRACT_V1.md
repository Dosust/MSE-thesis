# Stage 6 → Stage 7 Handoff Contract v1

## 1. Purpose

Stage 7 must receive qualified consequences, constraints, uncertainties and provenance — not an embedded Stage-6 recommendation.

## 2. Handoff object

\[
\boxed{
I_{6\to7}
=
\left(
\mathcal A_6^{qual},
\Delta\mathfrak R,
\Delta\mathbf J_{\rm sus},
\boldsymbol\eta_R,
\mathcal K_{\rm sus}^{cand},
\mathbb U_6,
\Lambda_6,
\Pi_6
\right)
}
\]

where:

- \(\mathcal A_6^{qual}\): physically/causally admissible interventions;
- \(\Delta\mathfrak R\): resilience-effect changes;
- \(\Delta\mathbf J_{\rm sus}\): separate sustainability consequence vectors;
- \(\boldsymbol\eta_R\): valid resource-efficiency diagnostics;
- \(\mathcal K_{\rm sus}^{cand}\): candidate hard/soft/reporting requirements with provenance;
- \(\mathbb U_6\): uncertainty/scenario representation;
- \(\Lambda_6\): validity and claim limits;
- \(\Pi_6\): provenance/evidence map.

## 3. Per-intervention record

Each \(a_k\) handed to Stage 7 must include:

1. intervention ID;
2. physical target/mechanism/location/magnitude/timing/service life;
3. upstream qualification status;
4. scenario/domain;
5. resilience change;
6. accessibility/social vector by declared group/area where available;
7. lifecycle energy;
8. lifecycle carbon;
9. lifecycle cost by perspective;
10. extended environmental/social metrics where qualified;
11. resource-efficiency diagnostics;
12. uncertainty intervals/scenario ranges;
13. applicable requirements and pass/fail/coverage;
14. data/factor versions;
15. validation status;
16. claim ceiling.

## 4. No hidden weighting

The handoff does not include a default weighted sustainability score.

\[
\boxed{
I_{6\to7}
\not\ni
w^\top\mathbf J_{\rm sus}
}
\]

unless a value/weight model has been explicitly authorized and is declared as decision input rather than physical evidence.

## 5. Requirement statuses

Each requirement is tagged:

- HARD-AUTHORITATIVE;
- HARD-ADOPTED-STAKEHOLDER;
- SOFT-TARGET;
- ENGINEERING-BENCHMARK;
- RESEARCH-SENSITIVITY;
- REPORT-ONLY.

Stage 7 may not silently promote a lower-authority class into a hard constraint.

## 6. Uncertainty handoff

Separate:

\[
\mathbb U_6
=
(
\mathbb U_{\rm aleatory},
\mathbb U_{\rm epistemic},
\mathbb U_{\rm model},
\mathbb U_{\rm numerical},
\mathbb U_{\rm normative}
).
\]

Only scientifically justified probabilities are passed as probabilities.

## 7. Stage-7 activation

Stage 7 may design its decision protocol before Stage-6 empirical execution is complete.

Final Stage-7 decision execution, however, may use only Stage-6 records whose required validity gates have passed.

## 8. Claim boundary

Stage 6 may state:

“these are the quantified consequences and qualified requirements.”

Stage 7 answers:

“which intervention or nondominated/robust set should be selected under the declared decision model?”
