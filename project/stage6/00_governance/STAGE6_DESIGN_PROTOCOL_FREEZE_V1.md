## H4 boundary

H4 tests whether at least two qualified resilience-effective interventions have materially different sustainability consequence vectors under the preregistered rule.

H4 does not assume that resilience improvements are sustainable, does not assume every intervention produces a trade-off, and does not rank interventions.

## Weighting boundary

No default single sustainability score is authorized.

Any later cross-dimensional weighting is a Stage-7 decision/value model and requires explicit provenance, not physical inference.
