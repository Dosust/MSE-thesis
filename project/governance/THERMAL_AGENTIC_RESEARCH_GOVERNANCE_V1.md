## Mandatory 16 rules
R01 AUTHORITY: record controlling file, revision/commit and conflict resolution.
R02 FREEZE: do not change frozen objectives, eight identifiers or I1–I6 meanings without authorized amendment.
R03 PHYSICS: begin with control volumes, conservation, admissible mechanisms and operating limits.
R04 EVIDENCE: distinguish published class mechanism (L), selection (S), equipment eligibility (E), independent validation (V).
R05 CAUSALITY: common forcing, generic electricity supply and co-evolving temperatures do not establish thermal directionality.
R06 FIDELITY: add states only if necessary, identifiable and pertinent to declared quantities of interest.
R07 MATHEMATICS: record units, sign conventions, energy residuals, initial and boundary conditions, parameter domains, and well-posedness assumptions.
R08 STATE/TIME: separate ODE states, algebraic ports, discrete modes, forcing, threshold time, actuation time, receiver response, shortfall and recovery.
R09 HYPOTHESES: preregister counterhypotheses, measurement positions, confounders and disconfirmation logic before testing.
R10 VVUQ: code verification ≠ independent physical validation; synthetic results ≠ field results.
R11 UNCERTAINTY: quantify uncertainty and failure to identify parameters; label results conditional.
R12 SERVICE: electricity/mobility required services govern resilience; efficiency, SOC/readiness alone do not prove resilience.
R13 NOVELTY: compare authoritative prior works, separate existing findings from proposals, do not claim novelty from search absence alone.
R14 INTEGRITY: no invented papers, parameters, events, measurements, standards clauses, approvals, or literature access.
R15 AUTHORIZATION: agents may investigate and draft; claim promotions, canonical revisions, merges, releases and external submissions need explicit authority and satisfied checks.
R16 AUDIT: immutable evidence/provenance and findings logs; use PRs, readbacks and independent reviewer signoff.

## Scientific stage gates
G0 Authority/scope; G1 source-specific evidence; G2 governing physics; G3 mathematical/model admission; G4 preregistered competing hypothesis; G5 numerical verification; G6 independent physical validation; G7 service and state-time interpretation; G8 reviewed Deck3/public release.
Each gate: PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED, NOT_TESTED, NOT_APPLICABLE with an artifact link, reviewer and rationale. PASS in one gate cannot imply PASS in another. G6 requires independent physical evidence, not agent consensus.

## Agent roles and non-circular review
A0 Governor resolves authority; A1 Literature verifies source identity/full text; A2 Physics derives mechanisms; A3 Mathematics formulates models; A4 Critic attempts falsification; A5 Computational Verifier runs checks; A6 Evidence Auditor tests admissibility/validation; A7 Deck3 communicates approved status; A8 Release Auditor checks PR and source/figure trace. Reviewer must not self-attest independent validation. Any role may return BLOCKED; no agent can create ASU or faculty approval.

## Execution and change control
Research exploration: autonomous in an active tool-enabled session, with provenance. Versioned candidate code and findings: reviewable branch and pull request. Canonical science amendment or release: authorized human review. External publication/IRB decisions: applicable institutional requirements.
Do not auto-merge when CI, reviews, equipment evidence or scientific release gates are absent. A green software workflow verifies only tests it actually ran.
Never fetch secrets, expose protected personal information, or transmit restricted data to external services without authorization.

## Research claim record
For each result record: claim ID, authority/file SHA, study interface/components, source DOI/page and scope, device/control volume, source→receiver ports and directions, governing equation and units, assumptions/parameters and bounds, hypothesis and alternative, code/data revision, tests and observed outcomes, uncertainty, independent validation domain, disposition, Deck3 usage permissions, and human reviewer.

Respect existing Deck3 palette and skill-specific controls rather than overriding them.
