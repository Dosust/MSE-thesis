## 1. Objective wording remains unchanged

The frozen General Objective and SO1–SO3 remain unchanged.

## 2. Sustainability relationship

The outputs of SO3 may support a bounded two-pathway sustainability assessment:

```text
Pathway 1
thermal interdependency
→ electricity / mobility service consequence
→ environmental + economic + social consequence

Pathway 2
engineering response
→ changed state / capability / service
→ environmental + economic + social consequence
```

This downstream assessment does not create SO4 and does not broaden the core thermal-interdependency objective contract.

## 3. SDG rule

Where sustainability indicators are measured, SDG relevance may be interpreted only as:

```text
Measured Sustainability Indicator
→ Sustainability Dimension
→ Relevant SDG Target / Indicator
```

Exact SDG targets remain open until the corresponding indicators are operationalized.


## 4. Placement rule

```text
Effectiveness → Resilience / Engineering-Response Assessment
Energy Efficiency → Environmental Sustainability
```

These are downstream interpretation rules. They do not create SO4 or alter the frozen GO and SO1–SO3.
