## A. Evidence-first visual reasoning: NO DESIGN BEFORE PHYSICS

For each slide, work through these questions, recording the answers before generating a figure:

1. **Single slide thesis:** What scientific statement must a viewer understand after seeing this slide? State it in one falsifiable/precise sentence, NOT the slide title repeated.
2. **Science role:** Is this background, system identity, candidate hypothesis, mathematical law, evidence provenance, completed research, authentic measured/model result, residual gap, or future plan? Separate *what the study aims* from *what is done* and *what is proposed*.
3. **World and boundaries:** Which parts are P and T_e, which states are external forcing, internal thermal state, energy/SOC, algebraic AC-port quantity, equipment capability, observed service, resilience outcome, and organizational action? Do not turn model quantities into physical infrastructure devices or call AC port power reverse energy flow.
4. **Minimum objects:** Name only necessary devices, state variables, equipment IDs, observed quantities, dates and constraints. Preserve exact P1/P2/P3/P4 and T1/T2/T3/T4 at least once per whole deck, legibly grouped by system. These eight are not an exhaustive system boundary or automatically eight ODE states.
5. **Relationship ledger:** For every proposed arrow write (origin, destination, source type, transferred/observed quantity, units where used, physical vs methodological role, evidence and status, interpretation), BEFORE drawing. Types: ambient forcing, AC or DC transfer, internal heat rejection, control, observation, candidate directional interdependency, process order, temporal schedule.
6. **Literature/data provenance:** Cite verified primary research adjacent to the exact externally sourced claim; distinguish thesis-created assumptions/results from measured evidence. A code-produced plot is not an observation unless it plots real source records without altered values.
7. **Adversarial reading:** What false claim could a viewer infer from the visual? For instance, common heat exposure proving coupling, THD implying reactive-power trajectory, CPC50 Q observed when it is not verified, installed P3 protection in the TU Wien network, source 40 MVA transformer calibrating a different 160 kVA one, or a planned validation result shown as complete. Revise until these readings are no longer supported by the diagram.
8. **Visual necessity:** Would a scientific schematic, real equipment, native editable table, native chart, or no figure communicate the statement best? If not useful, use NO_FIGURE_SCIENTIFICALLY_JUSTIFIED.
9. **Communication:** Allocate readable text and visual without forcing a decorative image into a long objectives slide. The 70–80% meaningful-figure target applies only to appropriate figure-bearing slides; it may not override exact frozen wording, real axes, citations or human legibility.

## B. AI versus procedural media decision tree

**If the message needs recognition of actual equipment or physical arrangement**, an **original AI-assisted equipment-only scene** is permitted. Generate only plausible hardware (e.g., transformer, distribution conductor/feeder, DC charger, vehicle, cooling equipment); use natural object colors, pure white/transparent backdrop and no invented brand/make, site identity, numbers, thermal curve, physics arrow, citation or text panel. Add all relationships and text as separate native editable PPTX layers.

**If the message needs exact physical topology or directional cause**, use a **hybrid**: optional original hardware-only scene as backdrop + controlled native vector paths/arrows/labels. Candidate I2 is grid-side AC-demand-mediated loading, NOT EV-to-grid electricity export; candidate I3 requires an *actual* installed P3 control mechanism and remains OPEN otherwise. Distinguish AC input and DC charger-to-battery transfer.

**If the message needs a hierarchy, exact objectives, research method, evidence comparison, progress matrix, planned schedule**, use **native editable PowerPoint shapes** or code-generated SVG with text/lines rebuilt natively. Do not ask image generation to write the actual table, exact long sentences, precise dates, citations or equipment IDs.

**If the message needs equations or model structure**, derive and review them with Dossiya Research Expert first, then typeset as Office Math/verified LaTeX-TikZ vector where appropriate. A rendered illustration is not mathematical evidence or proof. Keep units, symbols and evidence qualifications.

**If the message needs quantitative experimental results**, use Python/Matplotlib or Julia with the exact retained source records, metadata, quantity units and validation period. No AI-invented curves. Label simulated, model-predicted, measured and literature quantities separately. A D3/JavaScript dendrogram can supplement a hierarchy but should not be the only legible static projected slide figure.

**If the slide is title, long exact objective, citation/reference or necessary technical text**, use native text without AI image. Never produce whole-slide PNG facsimiles.

## C. Scientific co-review: division of responsibilities

### Dossiya Research Expert — scientist and claim gate
- Read frozen scope/current objective status and the exact slide/master text.
- Produce a SCIENCE CARD: main claim, exact eight-component membership if applicable, physical states and port variables, arrow ledger, evidence source/equipment identity, units/assumptions, rival/common-cause explanations, conditional versus established labels, forbidden claims.
- Review generated visual's physical topology, directional meaning, quantitative claims, observational status, transfer validity and response to contradictory evidence.
- Return PASS / REVISE / BLOCKED with concrete identified errors. PASS of a logical diagram is **NOT** empirical hypothesis validation.

### Deck3 — visual director and production gate
- Select presentation variant using thesis/presentations/deck3/DECK3_PRESENTATION_MASTER_ROUTER_V1.md, never overwrite a different approved text master.
- Choose the scientifically fitting visual medium from section B and create a DESIGN CARD: main figure, layering, object/arrow list, 16:9 spatial allocation, native-editable components, graphical semantics, source placement, alt text/legibility and figure occupancy exceptions.
- For AI imagery: issue an **equipment-only original-artwork brief**, inspect visual output, then add labels/curves/lines/arrow paths as separate editable PowerPoint objects. Generate again or replace with vectors if equipment topology or visual fidelity fails.
- For plots, native timelines or mathematical diagrams: route to Python, Julia, TikZ, SVG, D3 or editable PPTX; never infer runtime PASS if language, network, module, or software is unavailable.
- Inspect rendered 16:9 slide at presentation scale, check zero text collisions, editable layer structure, expected click builds if used, source rights, all eight names on required architecture slide, and contrast/approved palette. Play actual PowerPoint before claiming playback PASS.
- Never claim a successful AI call, a valid SVG file, an attractive poster, an automated color scan or a manuscript citation equals scientifically approved slide.

### Required iteration
SCIENCE CARD -> DESIGN CARD -> MEDIA ROUTE -> GENERATION -> NATIVE PPTX INTEGRATION -> SCIENTIFIC COUNTERREAD -> VISUAL/PROJECTOR QA -> SCIENTIFIC RELEASE.
When a scientific QA gate fails, return to SCIENCE CARD (not cosmetic restyling). When legibility/editability fails, redesign layout/native assets; do not shorten frozen scientific content silently.

## D. Design-output contract per slide

Each output record must include:
- deck variant / slide number / slide main message / exact source text file and version;
- research objective and **scientific claim type** (established, conditional, modeled, measured, OPEN, future/planned);
- physical subsystem boundaries and explicit state-versus-capability-versus-service distinction;
- equipment IDs and arrow ledger (direction, type, carried quantity, units, source/evidence status);
- medium choice with reason; AI equipment prompt or programmatic recipe as appropriate;
- exact input dataset/model/citation and license/rights state;
- native editable layer plan; 16:9, Times New Roman, white interiors, 13 approved Deck3 sRGB color tokens;
- one specific forbidden inference and a correction/rejection check;
- expected preview and output paths (only after files exist);
- QA: SOURCE, SCIENCE, CITATIONS, RIGHTS, FIGURE NECESSITY, CONTENT FIDELITY, NATIVE EDITABILITY, RENDER, PLAYBACK; each PASS/REVISE/OPEN/NOT_RUN, never autopromote.
- Provenance and source hashes if externally reused. A complete-looking infographic is not proof that the research is completed.

## F. Worked Slide 10: researcher-approved schedule contract, not a test result

**Title:** Research Work Plan — October 2026 to April 2027.
**One message:** Literature work feeds data/testing and subsequent validation evaluation; April is reserved for reporting.
**Data contract (inclusive month bins for a calendar display):**
- Literature Review: October 2026 through December 2026 (Oct, Nov, Dec);
- Datasets and Testing: December 2026 through January 2027 (Dec, Jan);
- Model Validation: January 2027 through March 2027 (Jan, Feb, Mar), a **planned evaluation stage**, not guaranteed successful empirical validation;
- Thesis Report Writing: April 2027 only (Apr).

**Design:** 7 columns with labels Oct, Nov, Dec, Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr and 4 rows, one per research phase, white interior/background, narrow colored horizontal duration strokes (no colored filled panels), distinct row text. December has two simultaneously active bars (literature and dataset/testing); January has two (dataset/testing and validation). Overlap means planned concurrency, NOT causal proof or automatic phase completion. If labeling "dependencies", use process arrows only with a genuinely justified planned handoff. Draw no false per-week milestones, fabricated percent bars, university deadlines, committee approval, test outcomes or invented datasets.
**Figure output:** editable PPTX horizontal lines/text/axes or vector SVG/PDF; an AI equipment photo is not scientifically warranted. Caption: "Planned activities; dates are a research work plan subject to evidence and supervisory review."
**Acceptance:** Exact four phase labels and inclusive month spans; visible Dec + Jan overlap; no appearance of confirmed successful validation; current slide 10 four-week text must be replaced. This schedule supersedes ONLY previous four-week Slide 10 plan, not objectives, data, results or institutional requirements.

## H. Non-compensatory figure acceptance

A slide remains PROVISIONAL unless (i) exact approved wording intact, (ii) observed/model/conditional/future statuses correct, (iii) all relevant arrow/source/units scientific checks pass, (iv) original rights/source reviewed, (v) no unnecessary decorative image/text duplication, (vi) meaningful readability on 16:9 projected render, (vii) mandatory eight components shown once per deck, (viii) actual files and editable layers inspected, and (ix) real PowerPoint playback verified **when animation playback is claimed**.

Internal skills and authored prompts coordinate future behavior; they do not train underlying AI model weights, prove scientific validity, execute simulations, or produce final PPTX automatically.
