# CLOSEST EXISTING PAPERS / REVIEWS TO THE FULL-CHAIN OBJECTIVE

No one item below is treated as a source for the complete six-chain architecture. Together they show why the literature is fragmented across physical, computational, resilience, lifecycle and societal perspectives.

1. **Davidson, Kendra, Starbird, Nozick & Ewing (2022)** — *Managing disaster risk associated with critical infrastructure systems: a system-level conceptual framework*. DOI `10.1080/10286608.2022.2067848`. Important bridge from physical systems and event occurrence through service interruption/provider/user response to societal-function deficits.

2. **Ji, He, Chen & Zhang (2024)** — *Computational methodologies for critical infrastructure resilience modeling: A review*. DOI `10.1016/j.aei.2024.102663`. Broad computational-method bridge covering network/topological, simulation, optimization, data-driven and physics-informed approaches.

3. **Wang, Zhao & Zhang (2024)** — *Multiplex networks in resilience modeling of critical infrastructure systems: A systematic review*. DOI `10.1016/j.ress.2024.110300`. Central for multiplex representation, attack/failure, resilience evaluation/evolution/control.

4. **Akiyama (2025)** — *Life-cycle approaches to sustainable and resilient infrastructure: Innovations in multi-hazard frameworks*. DOI `10.1080/15732479.2025.2524819`. Important lifecycle bridge among deterioration, multihazard resilience, adaptation and sustainability.

5. **Tong et al. (2025)** — interdependent-infrastructure resilience review. DOI `10.1016/j.ijcip.2025.100793`. Useful for system characteristics, assessment, resilience improvement, dynamic equilibrium and target trade-offs.

6. **Kumar & Hashem Mehany (2026)** — comprehensive review of infrastructure sustainability/resilience integration. DOI `10.1007/s43621-026-02973-9`. Important for definitions, quantification and integration of resilience with sustainability.

7. **Anvari & Timme (2026)** — *Sustainability as a challenge in complex systems dynamics*. DOI `10.1038/s43588-026-00971-5`. Important mathematical-physics bridge for sustainability in complex interconnected systems, including energy/mobility and combined theoretical/computational/data-driven approaches.

## Interpretation
The proposed review paper is strongest when framed as an ontology/integration review that asks which layers exist, which mathematics/physics operates at each layer, which cross-layer links are mature, and which links remain fragmented—not as an assertion that prior literature has already settled a six-chain sequence.
