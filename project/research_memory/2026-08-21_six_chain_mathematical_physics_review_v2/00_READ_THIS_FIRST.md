## Non-negotiable distinctions
- topology ≠ interdependency ≠ interface mechanism
- physical damage ≠ functionality ≠ delivered service ≠ societal consequence
- viability/reachability ≠ resilience metric
- resilience ≠ sustainability
- recovery/restoration is explicit
- optimization is cross-cutting, not a lifecycle stage
- societal evaluation is normative/institutional/behavioral, not a downstream physical state
- mathematics cuts across all six chains rather than becoming a seventh chain
- citations beside generic equations do not imply verbatim equation provenance
- the six chains are coupled loops, not one giant arrow
