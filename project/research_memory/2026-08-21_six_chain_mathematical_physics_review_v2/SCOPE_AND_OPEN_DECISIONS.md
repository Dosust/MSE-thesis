# SCOPE AND OPEN DECISIONS

## 1. Infrastructure-sector scope
Project history contains more than one scope state:
- earlier executable demonstrator: **Power–Water–Transport**, with Solid Waste treated as an extension;
- later conceptual thesis architecture: **Power–Water–Transportation–Solid Waste**.

**Status:** do not silently resolve from memory. Check the latest thesis charter/decision log before freezing the executable sector set.

## 2. Review-paper scope vs demonstrator scope
The six-chain ontology is deliberately broader than one case study. The review can map many infrastructure sectors even if the executable thesis demonstrator is narrower.

## 3. Meaning of “full chain”
“Full” means the broadest defensible literature-grounded ontology currently synthesized. It is a hypothesis for systematic review, not an immutable taxonomy. Layers may be added, split, merged or renamed if the evidence supports it.

## 4. Mathematics vs physics
Physics supplies governing laws, constraints and phenomena; mathematics supplies representations, inference, dynamics, optimization, control and decision methods across all chains. Their prominence need not be equal in every layer.

## 5. Literature verification status
Foundational DOI anchors are strong, but the final publication bibliography must still undergo source-level bibliographic verification and equation-level verification before direct quotation/attribution.
