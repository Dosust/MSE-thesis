# Frozen Scientific Distinctions and Corrections

## D1 — Six chains, not one giant arrow
The system contains distinct analytical logics with feedback. A visually linear presentation can falsely imply that societal choice is a downstream physical state.

## D2 — Topology vs interdependency vs interface
**Topology ≠ interdependency ≠ interface mechanism.**
A network edge does not by itself define transfer law, direction, capacity, delay, threshold, or failure mechanism.

## D3 — Physical state vs functionality vs service vs consequence
**Physical damage ≠ component functionality ≠ delivered service ≠ societal consequence.**
Use explicit mappings `X → F → S → C`.

## D4 — Recovery is explicit
Recovery/restoration must not disappear inside the word “resilience.” It has its own dynamics, sequencing, repair-resource allocation, and interdependency effects.

## D5 — Viability and reachability are not resilience metrics
Viability/reachability answer whether admissible/safe states can be maintained or reached; resilience metrics summarize disturbance/recovery performance. They interact but are not synonyms.

## D6 — Optimization is cross-cutting
Optimization is a mathematical methodology acting on design, operation, control, recovery, maintenance, planning, adaptation, and societal trade-offs. It is not a lifecycle stage.

## D7 — Sustainability is not resilience
Resilience concerns persistence, absorption, recovery, adaptation, etc. Sustainability evaluates lifecycle environmental/economic/social trajectories. The review should model their intersection without collapsing the concepts.

## D8 — Society is not a physical subsystem stage
Equity, economics/finance, governance, human behavior, and decision-making are evaluative, normative, behavioral, and institutional dimensions surrounding/acting on the physical system.

## D9 — Established literature vs synthesis
No claim should be made that a single prior paper proposes the complete six-chain ontology. The novelty is systematic synthesis/integration of recurrent literature-grounded layers.

## D10 — Equation provenance
Never imply a generic canonical formula is a verbatim equation from an anchor paper unless verified against that source.
