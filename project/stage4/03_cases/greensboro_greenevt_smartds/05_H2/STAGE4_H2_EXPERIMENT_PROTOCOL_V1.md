# Stage 4 H2 Experiment Protocol v1

## Hypothesis

Under matched physical states and disturbances, prespecified changes in information availability and organizational conditions produce measurable differences in estimation quality, feasible control, response timing, service trajectories, recovery, and downstream Stage-5 resilience outcomes.

## Nested architectures

\`\`\`math
\mathscr N_0=P+T,\qquad
\mathscr N_1=P+T+I,\qquad
\mathscr N_2=P+T+I+O.
\`\`\`

\(\mathscr N_0\) is an idealized/reference knowledge-control architecture, not a claim that real infrastructure operates without information. \(\mathscr N_1-\mathscr N_0\) isolates information/observation/estimation effects. \(\mathscr N_2-\mathscr N_1\) isolates organizational/coordination effects.

\`\`\`math
\boxed{Y_0,\eta,\mathbb I^{qualified},\theta_P,\theta_T,\omega\text{ matched across contrasts}.}
