## Decision scope and interpretation

- The eight are selected **physical-component/realized-hardware research subjects**, not eight cross-system interfaces and not automatically eight independently necessary thermal differential equations.
- The study is centered on qualifying thermal interdependencies **across the two system boundaries**, including the components' internal thermal/electrical/operating mechanisms and effects on required electricity and mobility services.
- The selection is based on literature-supported physical roles and the designated research quantities: EVSE charging efficiency, nonoverlapping electricity losses, mobility energy consumption per measured distance where available, mobility readiness, service-based resilience, and bounded two-pathway sustainability consequences.
- This is **component-selection authority for the working research programme**, not a declaration that any equipment-specific parameter or bidirectional thermally causal coupling has already passed experimental validation.
- **Classification:** P1/P2/T1 are central to electrical transfer and thermal operating behavior; P3 conditionally executes thermal-related restrictions; P4 and T3 govern within-system heat rejection; T2 stores energy and maps charging to readiness; T4 influences hot-weather vehicle energy needs. Not every component directly spans the intersystem interface.
- Equipment-specific model fidelity, parameter identification, observable operating rules, actual P→T and T→P causal pathways, case validation, independent necessity tests and final state count are subsequent scientific tasks. The user-selected eight **remain the investigation roster** unless explicitly revised.
- The Electrified Transportation System boundary is not reduced to these four model candidates. The underlying full definition remains unchanged.
