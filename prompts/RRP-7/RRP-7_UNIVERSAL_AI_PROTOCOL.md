# RRP-7 — Resilience Reasoning Protocol, 7-Stage

## Canonical name
**RRP-7 — Resilience Reasoning Protocol, 7-Stage**

## Short call name
**RRP-7**

## Purpose
RRP-7 is a vendor-neutral reasoning protocol for rigorous thesis analysis, scientific infographics, resilience modeling, interdependency reasoning, and engineering decision support.

It is designed so the same instruction can be used with any capable AI system.

## Core invariant

> No figure without meaning.  
> No arrow without mechanism.  
> No resilience without purpose.  
> No conclusion without evidence.  
> No analysis without a decision consequence.

## Seven reasoning stages

1. **Structure** — What exists, what can interact, and what is known?
2. **Causality** — Why and through what mechanism can effects propagate?
3. **Dynamics** — How and when does the coupled system evolve?
4. **Observation & Control** — What can be observed, inferred, coordinated, and acted upon?
5. **Viability** — Can acceptable service survive or recover under constraints?
6. **Sustainability Translation** — What long-term sustainability requirements follow from the resilience result?
7. **Transformation** — What should change, where, when, and in what sequence?

## Mandatory critical-thinking chain

Every figure, claim, model element, result, or proposed intervention must answer:

**WHAT → WHY → HOW → SO WHAT → WHAT DO WE DO?**

- **WHAT** — What system, state, dependency, failure, result, or decision object is being represented?
- **WHY** — Why does it matter for resilience, service, recovery, sustainability, or transformation?
- **HOW** — Through what physical, informational, organizational, mathematical, or control mechanism does it occur?
- **SO WHAT** — What consequence follows for propagation, service, viability, uncertainty, recovery, or sustainability?
- **WHAT DO WE DO** — What engineering, modeling, monitoring, control, planning, or research decision follows?

## Mandatory evidence gate for dependencies

No dependency or interdependency is accepted without:

1. **Direction**
2. **Mechanism**
3. **Component mapping**
4. **Dependency type**
5. **Evidence/provenance**
6. **Boundary of validity**

A shared hazard or spatial co-exposure must not be treated automatically as a causal dependency.

## Mandatory resilience-purpose gate

Whenever the word **resilience** is used, specify:

- resilience **of what system**;
- resilience **to what disturbance**;
- resilience **of which service/function**;
- resilience **over what time horizon**;
- resilience **under what constraints**;
- resilience **for which users/stakeholders**, when relevant;
- resilience **for what engineering or decision purpose**.

A resilience metric without these items is incomplete.

## Artifact completeness model

Every thesis artifact should contain, explicitly or implicitly:

**A = {Q, O, M, D, C, A_c, E, B}**

where:

- **Q** = question
- **O** = object/system
- **M** = mechanism
- **D** = dynamics
- **C** = consequence
- **A_c** = action/decision consequence
- **E** = evidence
- **B** = boundary of validity

## Hard-fail conditions

Revise the artifact if any of the following occurs:

- decorative visual with no analytical purpose;
- arrow labeled only as “interaction” or “interdependency” without a mechanism;
- correlation or co-exposure presented as causality;
- resilience presented as a score without defining its purpose and service meaning;
- structure, causality, dynamics, control, viability, sustainability, and transformation mixed without distinction;
- result presented without interpretation;
- interpretation presented without evidence;
- recommendation presented without linking it to a diagnosed mechanism or constraint;
- sustainability inferred merely because recovery occurred;
- organizational or information domains shown as passive decoration rather than functional mechanisms.

## Universal invocation — short

**Apply RRP-7 to this material. Audit meaning, mechanism, evidence, resilience purpose, stage correctness, interpretation, and engineering decision consequence. Reject decorative or unsupported reasoning.**

## Universal invocation — rigorous

**Apply RRP-7 (Resilience Reasoning Protocol, 7-Stage) to the material below. Evaluate it through Structure → Causality → Dynamics → Observation & Control → Viability → Sustainability Translation → Transformation. For every important figure, arrow, claim, model relation, result, or intervention, require WHAT → WHY → HOW → SO WHAT → WHAT DO WE DO. Do not accept a dependency without direction, mechanism, component mapping, type, evidence, and boundary of validity. Do not accept “resilience” unless the system, disturbance, service, time horizon, constraints, affected users where relevant, and decision purpose are defined. Distinguish common-cause exposure from causal dependency. Identify hard failures first, then provide precise corrections and the engineering implication.**
