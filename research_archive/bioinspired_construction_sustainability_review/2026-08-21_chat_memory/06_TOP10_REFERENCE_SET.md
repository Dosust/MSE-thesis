## Fair paper-by-paper assessment principle established in chat

For each paper:

1. understand the authors' own objective;
2. evaluate scientific/methodological quality separately from usefulness to the review;
3. inspect engineering evidence;
4. inspect sustainability evidence;
5. inspect the engineering-to-sustainability integration mechanism;
6. inspect decision consequence;
7. inspect reproducibility;
8. inspect validation;
9. inspect assumptions/uncertainty;
10. classify strengths and weaknesses only from evidence;
11. compare papers only after individual dossiers are frozen.

Weaknesses were to be typed as:

- W1 — author-acknowledged limitation;
- W2 — evidence-supported methodological limitation;
- W3 — review-specific limitation.

Strengths were to be distinguished as scientific/design, evidence/transparency, sustainability-assessment, integration, decision, and transferability strengths.
