## Important methodological correction from the re-audit

Many of the 26 papers previously appeared in visual/synthesis tables as if they contributed sustainability-assessment evidence. The stricter re-audit separates:

- bio-inspired design/review frameworks;
- engineering-performance simulation/testing;
- sustainability discussion;
- direct focal-author sustainability-assessment implementation;
- direct method-generated quantified sustainability outputs.

This produces many legitimate failures of L and M without implying the papers are scientifically poor.
