### M1

Executable package: [`models/H1/M1/`](models/H1/M1/).  
Status: **verified — 13 M1-specific tests passed, 0 failed**.

M1 preserves the verified M0 radial-power and CTM transport solvers and adds only memoryless algebraic interface maps:

- same-step transport demand creates charger arrival demand;
- requested EV charging power maps exactly once into power-bus demand;
- bus energization limits same-step served charger power;
- mobility service is algebraically derated by same-step charging fulfillment;
- repair access is a fixed snapshot delay only.

There is deliberately no charger queue state, no evolving interface state and no dynamic repair-resource delay in M1.

A key EGMIV correction made before freeze was to distinguish **requested** EV charging power from **served** charger power. During a bus outage, requested EV electricity remains in the power-service denominator rather than disappearing. The deterministic outage benchmark therefore gives minimum $S_P=0.4736842105$ rather than the invalid 0.60 accounting value.

M1 passed dimensional identity, exact load mapping, monotonicity, zero-demand, sufficient-capacity, static-repair-access, time-step refinement, no-double-counting, outage-demand accounting, and strict memorylessness gates. The decoupling control satisfies
