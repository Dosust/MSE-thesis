## Required paper-audit procedure

For each candidate:

1. Verify exact title and DOI against the PDF.
2. Confirm full-text identity/status.
3. Read relevant Methods, Results, Validation, tables, figures, appendices, and supplementary evidence when present.
4. Extract sustainability-method evidence independently of current spreadsheet wording.
5. Extract methodological specification/reproducibility evidence.
6. Extract author-generated engineering evidence.
7. Extract sustainability outputs separately.
8. Identify the exact engineering–sustainability linkage.
9. Code provenance.
10. Assign R1,R2,R3,R4,L.
11. Apply caps and route logic.
12. Record exact evidence passage and page/section/table/figure.
13. Self-audit all scores of 3.
14. Conduct second review or mark unresolved.
15. Only then score relevance and quality.

- Paper ID

- Confidence: High/Medium/Low
- Second-review status

## Reproducibility safeguards

- Do not infer missing method components merely because they are typical of LCA, AHP, MIVES, MCDA, ML, or another method.
- Mark missing information `NR`.
- Mark genuine ambiguity `U`.
- Preserve author terminology.
- Keep exact values/units.
- Do not treat a literature-review statement as focal-author evidence.
- Do not treat supplier values as author-generated engineering results.
- Do not equate sensitivity analysis with validation automatically.
- Do not use relevance scores to repair methodological failure.
- Do not stop when a desired sample size is reached.
