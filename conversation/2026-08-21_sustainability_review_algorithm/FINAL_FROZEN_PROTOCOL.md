# Final Frozen Protocol

## 1. Two-axis model

For paper i, methodological eligibility and review relevance are separate.

Evidence vector:

`E_i = (R1_i, R2_i, R3_i, R4_i, L_i)`

with each component in {0,1,2,3}.

Relevance vector:

`Z_i = (Z1_i, Z2_i, Z3_i, Z4_i, Z5_i)`

with each component in {0,1,2,3}.

Governing principle:

> Extraction determines scoring; scoring must never determine extraction.

## 2. R1 — Sustainability-method implementation

- 0: no sustainability assessment.
- 1: informal, qualitative, or under-specified sustainability calculation.
- 2: recognizable structured method, but incomplete/unclear implementation.
- 3: structured sustainability method directly implemented and producing results.

R1=3 requires an identifiable method, implementation by focal authors, defined inputs, generated outputs, and evidence beyond literature-review mention.

## 3. R2 — Method specification and reproducibility

Applicable specification components may include:

`M_i=(FU,SB,INV,DB,SW,SL,AL,PROC,W,N,UNC)`

where FU=functional unit, SB=system boundary, INV=inventory/input data, DB=database, SW=software, SL=service life, AL=allocation/cut-off, PROC=procedure, W=weighting, N=normalization, UNC=uncertainty/sensitivity.

Not every component applies to every method.

Coverage ratio:

`RC_i = adequately_reported_applicable_components / total_applicable_components`

Guide:

- R2=0 if RC<0.25;
- R2=1 if 0.25<=RC<0.50;
- R2=2 if 0.50<=RC<0.75;
- R2=3 if RC>=0.75.

LCA caps:

- FU missing OR SB missing -> R2<=2.
- FU missing AND SB missing -> R2<=1.

For MCDA/index/ML studies, use equivalent essential components such as criteria, alternatives, weights, normalization, aggregation, data, model structure, decision rules, validation, and sensitivity.

## 4. R3 — Author-generated engineering performance

- 0: no engineering-performance evidence.
- 1: assumed, qualitative, supplier-derived, cited, or design-condition-only performance.
- 2: author-generated quantitative evidence but incomplete reporting/validation.
- 3: direct and sufficiently reported experimental, monitored, analytical, or validated simulated engineering evidence.

R3=3 requires focal-author generation, identifiable method, focal alternative/system, engineering/functional metric, quantitative result, unit, and adequate method reporting.

R3=2 is allowed only when the evidence is author-generated and the weakness is reporting completeness. Secondary evidence cannot receive R3=2.

## 5. R4 — Quantified sustainability outcomes

- 0: no sustainability output.
- 1: qualitative/indirect/weakly specified sustainability claim.
- 2: valid numerical output with limited coverage or incomplete specification.
- 3: clear quantitative outcomes generated by the implemented sustainability method.

R4=3 requires structured method, quantitative indicator, interpretable unit/score, comparator/baseline/alternatives, and reported result. It does not require all environmental/economic/social dimensions.

## 6. L — Engineering–sustainability linkage

- L=0: one domain absent, different systems, or no relationship.
- L=1: engineering and sustainability are generated for the same alternatives but reported in parallel.
- L=2: engineering performance constrains, defines, modifies, or informs sustainability assessment.
- L=3: explicit mathematical or decision-model integration.

Examples:

Parallel:

`(strength_i, GWP_i)` for the same alternatives, without integration -> L=1.

Analytical dependence:

`strength_i >= strength_min` before sustainability comparison; service life changes replacement frequency or annualized impact -> L=2.

Explicit integration:

`CI_i = GWP_i / f_c_i`

`EEI_i = EE_i / f_c_i`

`CE_i = Cost_i / f_c_i`

`SI_i = sum_j w_j z_ij` where engineering and sustainability variables enter the same model -> L=3.

## 7. Provenance coding

Use:

- G: generated by focal authors;
- I: implemented by focal authors;
- A: established external method/database applied;
- V: validated by focal authors;
- M: mentioned/discussed;
- C: cited/secondary;
- NR: not reported;
- U: unclear;
- NA: not applicable.

Evidence provenance is not methodological quality.

## 8. Non-compensatory eligibility routes

Tier A — Core integrated:

`R1=3 AND R2>=2 AND R3=3 AND R4=3 AND L>=2`

Tier B1 — Complete but parallel:

`R1=3 AND R2>=2 AND R3=3 AND R4=3 AND L=1`

Tier B2 — Engineering under-reported:

`R1=3 AND R2>=2 AND R3=2 AND R4=3 AND L>=2`

Tier B3 — Sustainability coverage limited:

`R1=3 AND R2>=2 AND R3=3 AND R4=2 AND L>=2`

Combined analytical eligibility:

`E*_i = A_i OR B1_i OR B2_i OR B3_i`

## 9. One-relaxation rule

Core threshold vector:

`tau=(3,2,3,3,2)`

Define deficiency indicator:

`d_ij = 1[E_ij < tau_j]`

and:

`D_i = sum_j d_ij`

An Extended paper may have only one controlled limitation. Therefore D_i=1 for B1/B2/B3.

Examples:

- (3,2,2,3,2) -> B2
- (3,2,3,2,2) -> B3
- (3,2,3,3,1) -> B1
- (3,2,2,2,2) -> Exclude

Absolute analytical exclusions include R1<=2, R2<=1, R3<=1, R4<=1, or L=0.

## 10. Relevance scoring

Five criteria:

- Z1 construction application;
- Z2 engineering-performance relevance;
- Z3 sustainability-assessment relevance;
- Z4 integration relevance;
- Z5 framework transferability.

Each is 0–3.

Raw score:

`RS_i = sum_k Z_ki`, 0<=RS<=15.

Weighted score:

`WRS_i = 0.15 Z1 + 0.20 Z2 + 0.20 Z3 + 0.25 Z4 + 0.20 Z5`

Interpretation:

- WRS>=2.50: High relevance
- 2.00<=WRS<2.50: Moderate-high
- 1.25<=WRS<2.00: Contextual
- WRS<1.25: Low

Weights must be frozen before scoring all papers.

Final analytical-use decision can use a predetermined relevance threshold r_min, initially 2.00:

`F_i = E*_i AND 1[WRS_i >= r_min]`

## 11. Quality score

For 12 binary quality criteria q_ik in {0,1}:

`Q_i = sum_k q_ik`, 0<=Q<=12.

Criteria:

1. primary data;
2. functional-unit/equivalent-basis clarity;
3. system-boundary clarity;
4. adequate engineering method;
5. replication/sample adequacy;
6. sensitivity/uncertainty;
7. model calibration;
8. external validation;
9. database/software reporting;
10. statistical analysis;
11. transparent limitations;
12. reproducibility.

Q ranks papers within the same tier/relevance category; it cannot override eligibility.

## 12. Paper evidence record

For every candidate extract:

- identity/title/DOI/year/journal;
- study design and construction object;
- sustainability method and exact R1 evidence;
- R2 configuration and score-cap reasons;
- engineering method/metric/unit/value/comparator/standard/validation and exact R3 evidence;
- sustainability outcomes/units/comparator and exact R4 evidence;
- linkage mechanism/formula and exact L evidence;
- provenance codes;
- R1,R2,R3,R4,L;
- route/tier;
- Z1–Z5, RS, WRS;
- Q;
- exact passage/page/section/table/figure;
- confidence;
- second-review status;
- final justification.

## 13. Dynamic evidence states

`S0 candidate identified`

`S1 PDF identity/full text confirmed`

`S2 evidence extracted`

`S3 provenance classified`

`S4 R1-R4-L scored`

`S5 route assigned`

`S6 relevance scored`

`S7 quality audited`

`S8 second review`

`S9 final analytical decision`

Only ordered transitions are permitted:

`S0 -> S1 -> S2 -> S3 -> S4 -> S5 -> S6 -> S7 -> S8 -> S9`

## 14. Conjectures to test, not assume

- H1: N(R3=3) > N(R4=3)
- H2: N(L=1) > N(L>=2)
- H3: eligible integrated evidence is more concentrated at material scale than building/system scale
- H4: environmental outputs > economic outputs > social outputs
- H5: integration probability is higher when R2=3 than when R2=2

## 15. Synthesis metrics

Integration rate:

`IR = N(L>=2) / N(R3>=2 AND R4>=2) * 100%`

Parallel-evidence rate:

`PER = N(L=1) / N(R3>=2 AND R4>=2) * 100%`

Direct-evidence rate:

`DER = (N_G + N_I) / N_all_coded_evidence_items * 100%`

Validation rate:

`VR = N_validated_methods_or_frameworks / N_implemented_methods_or_frameworks * 100%`

## 16. Perfect score versus Tier A

Perfect methodological vector:

`(3,3,3,3,3)` with total 15/15.

Tier A is broader and includes:

- (3,2,3,3,2)
- (3,2,3,3,3)
- (3,3,3,3,2)
- (3,3,3,3,3)

Do not call any observed paper perfect until paper-level evidence has been populated and audited.
