# Decisions and Corrections

## Corrections that must be remembered

1. **216 is not the number of available full PDFs.** It is the earlier eligible-paper set. The original available full-text corpus contains 202 papers.
2. **Five later research PDFs were added**, giving 207 available full papers for the strict full-text protocol.
3. **Bio-inspiration must not be a mandatory gate for the general 207-paper construction-sustainability corpus.** It is a separate characteristic/subreview gate. General review screening is construction relevance + framework/evaluation relevance.
4. **Tier A is not the same as perfect score.** Tier A permits four vectors: (3,2,3,3,2), (3,2,3,3,3), (3,3,3,3,2), (3,3,3,3,3). Only (3,3,3,3,3) is a perfect 15/15 methodological vector.
5. **Use L, not lambda**, for the engineering–sustainability linkage variable in the frozen notation.
6. **No paper should be called perfect merely because the logic admits (3,3,3,3,3).** Observed perfect papers require populated paper-level evidence and audit.
7. **A case study is application, not automatically validation.** Validation needs comparison, calibration, external testing, statistical validation, expert verification, sensitivity/uncertainty evidence, or equivalent support.
8. **Engineering performance and sustainability outcomes must remain separate.** GWP, cost, and sustainability scores are not R3 engineering performance. Strength, durability, thermal, service-life, structural, pavement, and functional metrics belong to R3.
9. **Secondary performance values cannot support R3=2 or 3.** R3=2 still requires author-generated quantitative evidence; its weakness is reporting completeness only.
10. **Relevance cannot repair failed eligibility.** The review uses two axes: methodological eligibility first, then review relevance.
11. **Total score does not determine eligibility.** It may rank papers within a tier only.
12. **The final number of papers must not be forced to equal 30 or another target.** The number must emerge from the frozen rules.
13. **The 31-paper list is a candidate-validation pool, not an automatically eligible final set.**
14. Earlier lists of 19 Tier A papers were provisional and contained one disputed substitution: the hybrid modular-steel-frame paper versus the construction-waste shading-device paper. The frozen protocol requires both to be re-audited rather than forcing one into the nineteenth position.
15. Three candidate papers were provisionally upgraded in one reconciliation but must still be verified under the frozen protocol: ferronickel slag, binary-blended concrete/MARS-SC, and design-based pavement LCA.

## Final decision architecture

- Evidence extraction precedes scores.
- Provenance is coded independently from quality.
- R1–R4–L produce a methodological route.
- Route can be A, B1, B2, B3, or Exclude.
- Relevance Z1–Z5 is scored only after methodological route assignment.
- Quality Q ranks papers but cannot override a gate.
- Every final score must retain an exact passage and location.
