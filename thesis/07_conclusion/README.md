# Chapter 7 — Conclusions and Future Work

## Purpose

Close every research question and hypothesis with a conclusion no stronger than the supporting evidence permits.

## Required sections

1. answers to research questions;
2. H1–H5 outcomes;
3. theoretical contribution;
4. methodological contribution;
5. computational/modeling contribution;
6. sustainable-engineering contribution;
7. practical engineering contribution;
8. limits of generalization;
9. future work.

## Closure rule

```math
\boxed{
\text{Conclusion}
\Rightarrow
\text{registered result}
+
\text{qualified evidence}
+
\text{declared validity domain}.
}
```

No `[P]` construct may be rewritten as a demonstrated contribution unless its corresponding validation and hypothesis chain closes.

## Future-work rule

Unvalidated extensions — including transfer to additional infrastructure sectors — remain explicit future work unless tested within the thesis.
