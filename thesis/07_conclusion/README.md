## Purpose

Close RQ0, RQ1–RQ7 and H1–H5 with conclusions no stronger than the supporting evidence, experiment, uncertainty analysis and validation domain permit.

2. answers to RQ1–RQ7;

5. methodological contribution;
6. computational/modeling contribution;

## RQ closure matrix

Every numbered research question must close through:

```math
\boxed{
RQ_i
\rightarrow
\text{result / evidence IDs}
\rightarrow
\text{supported answer}
\rightarrow
\text{validity domain}
\rightarrow
\text{unresolved remainder}.
}
```

RQ1/RQ2 may close through qualified representation/mechanism evidence plus downstream consistency tests; RQ3–RQ7 also report their corresponding H1–H5 outcomes.

## Closure rule

```math
\boxed{
\text{Conclusion}
\Rightarrow
\text{registered result/evidence}
+
\text{uncertainty}
+
\text{declared validity domain}.
}
```

No `[P]` construct may be rewritten as a demonstrated contribution unless its corresponding evidence, experiment, uncertainty and validation chain closes.

## Contribution rule

Separate contribution categories and state the support level for each. Do not use “novel,” “validated,” “superior,” or equivalent language unless the relevant novelty/validation/comparator gate has passed.

Interface criticality remains a supporting diagnostic unless separately tested. Adaptive/sequential transformation remains a supporting Stage-7 extension unless separately activated and validated.

## Future-work rule

Future work must arise from a concrete limitation, unresolved uncertainty, untested mechanism, restricted validation domain or unactivated extension. Transfer to additional infrastructure sectors remains future work unless tested within the thesis.

## Exit gate

See `../00_governance/THESIS_COMPLETION_GATES.md`. Chapter 7 is complete only when every RQ and hypothesis status is explicitly closed and no conclusion exceeds the registered scientific support.
