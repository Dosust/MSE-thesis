## Scientific and evidence guardrails

- **Extreme heat is a common external forcing, not proof of a directional thermal interdependency.**
- Normal AC supply `P1 → P2 → T1` and DC charging `T1 → T2` are electrical pathways; they do not establish candidate `I2/H1` or `I3/H2` thermal causality.
- The TU Wien network model, Bærug 40 MVA transformer predictive assessment, and Qatar charger field study refer to **different installations/evidence streams**. Do not present them as one observed coupled testbed.
- Bærug 0.907 K held-out MAE is an individual-transformer predictive assessment, not integrated P–T_e validation.
- Qatar charger 86/169-minute comparison comes from published external field evidence and must be shown as such.
- Required electricity/mobility service resilience and both sustainability pathways are an **assessment plan**, not demonstrated measured outcomes.
- No novel scientific mechanism, integrated physical validation, or SDG achievement is claimed by deck creation.
