## 1.12 Proposed Contributions

The proposed contributions are bounded by the thermal-only scope and remain conditional until supported by completed evidence and validation.

### C1 — Thermal-interdependency qualification

A reproducible distinction between common heat exposure and a directional thermal Power–Electrified Transportation interdependency, including mechanism, thermal state/capability, direction, and validity conditions.

### C2 — Physics-based state-time representation

A minimum-sufficient physics representation linking thermally active Power states, Electrified-Transportation states, and cross-system coupling quantities through time.

### C3 — Timing and service-consequence characterization

A quantified characterization of critical thermal transition time, interdependency propagation delay, and resulting electricity and mobility service trajectories where these quantities are identifiable.

The contribution chain is:

```text
Qualify thermal mechanism
→ Model state-time evolution
→ Quantify service consequence
```

The thesis does not claim contribution from broad Information/Organization modeling, generic viability theory, comprehensive sustainability optimization, or universal infrastructure decision frameworks.
