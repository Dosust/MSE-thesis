# 1.9 Research Questions

The general objective in Section 1.8 and the overarching research question RQ0 express the same thesis-level research destination in declarative and interrogative form, respectively. The wording below is reproduced from \`project/CANONICAL_RQ_SO_HYPOTHESES_V3.md\` without modification. RQ1–RQ7 decompose RQ0 into the ordered scientific questions required by the seven-stage research architecture.

## RQ0 — Overarching research question

> **How can coupled Power–Transportation infrastructure be represented as an interdependent System of Systems and modeled as a spatiotemporal complex dynamical system to quantify the effects of causal interdependencies, information and organizational constraints, uncertainty-aware service maintainability and recovery, and the resilience–sustainability consequences of engineering interventions?**

## RQ1 — System structure

> **What entities exist, what layers constitute the system, and where can interactions occur?**

## RQ2 — Causal mechanisms

> **Why and through which mechanisms do components and layers affect each other?**

## RQ3 — Hybrid dynamics

> **How, when, and where do accepted interdependency mechanisms evolve and propagate through the coupled system?**

## RQ4 — Observation, organization and control

> **How is the system observed, interpreted, coordinated and controlled?**

## RQ5 — Viability, reachability and interface diagnostics

> **Under what conditions can critical coupled services remain acceptable or recover under feasible controls and uncertainty, and what additional engineering information do viability and reachability provide?**

## RQ6 — Sustainability requirements and intervention consequences

> **What sustainability requirements follow from resilience diagnosis and from the effectiveness, resource efficiency, and sustainability consequences of candidate interventions?**

## RQ7 — Engineering decision and transformation

> **What should be changed, where, when and in what sequence to achieve effective, efficient and sustainable infrastructure transformation under feasibility constraints and uncertainty?**

The arrows denote dependency of the scientific argument: downstream questions rely on qualified outputs from upstream questions. They do **not** imply that the physical infrastructure itself evolves through seven discrete stages. Each RQ1–RQ7 is paired one-to-one with SO1–SO7 in Section 1.10; hypothesis-bearing comparisons are defined separately in Section 1.11.
