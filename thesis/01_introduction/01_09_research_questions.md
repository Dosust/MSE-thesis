## 1.9 Research Questions

**Current status:** the final wording of the research questions is **OPEN**, but their scope is permanently constrained by the thermal-only thesis authorities.

All final research questions must remain within:

```text
thermal interdependency
+ physical state
+ time
+ electricity/mobility consequence
```

The working question structure is:

### Working RQ1 — Thermal mechanisms

> **Which thermally sensitive physical states and directional thermal interdependency mechanisms connect the Power System and the Electrified Transportation System under extreme heat?**

### Working RQ2 — State-time evolution

> **How do qualified thermal interdependencies evolve and propagate through physical state and time under extreme heat?**

### Working RQ3 — Consequences

> **What critical transition times, propagation delays, and electricity and mobility service consequences result from those thermal interdependencies?**

These are **working formulations, not frozen wording**. Final research-question wording must be separately versioned and cannot broaden the thesis beyond the frozen General Objective and SO1–SO3.

The former RQ0 + RQ1–RQ7 architecture is historical/provenance material for the active thesis where it conflicts with the thermal-only scope.
