## 1.5 Sustainable Engineering Significance

The Sustainable Engineering significance of the thesis lies in improving the physical understanding of how extreme heat can propagate through coupled electricity and electrified-mobility infrastructure.

A system may appear adequate when Power and Electrified Transportation are assessed independently, yet a thermally conditioned dependency can change the timing or magnitude of service degradation. Identifying these pathways can support more targeted engineering design, adaptation, and recovery decisions.

Sustainability is not treated as a separate broad optimization domain in the active thesis. Environmental, economic, or resource consequences enter only if they are directly required to interpret a bounded thermal-resilience action and are supported by an explicit accounting boundary.

The primary contribution remains:

```text
thermal mechanism
→ state-time understanding
→ electricity/mobility service consequence
→ better-informed resilient engineering
```
