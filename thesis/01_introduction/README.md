This shorthand is only a communication projection. The active scientific semantics remain:

- Stage 5 = viability + reachability + probability/confidence;
- Stage 6 = diagnosis + intervention + effectiveness + efficiency + sustainability consequences;
- Stage 7 = engineering decision / transformation under feasibility and uncertainty.

Seven questions do not require seven hypotheses.

## Evidence discipline

- factual background statements require qualified adjacent citations in final thesis prose;
- gap claims must be traceable to Chapter-II synthesis and the separate gap/novelty audit;
- `[H]`, `[P]`, `[A]` and `[U]` statements must not be written as established facts;
- proposal-stage quantitative result language is prohibited unless an already executed method-development/verification result is explicitly identified as `[R]`;
- intervention effectiveness, efficiency and sustainability must remain conceptually distinct;
- interface criticality is a supporting diagnostic unless separately tested;
- adaptive/sequential transformation is conditional unless separately operationalized and preregistered.
