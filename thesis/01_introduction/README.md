# Chapter 1 — Introduction

## Purpose

Establish the engineering problem, significance, scope, research questions, objectives, hypotheses and proposed contribution without presenting unverified results.

## Required sections

1. Background and engineering context
2. Problem statement
3. Sustainable-engineering significance
4. Scope and system boundary
5. Research aim
6. Research questions
7. Research objectives
8. H_SYS and H1–H5
9. Proposed/expected contributions
10. Thesis organization

## Governing logic

```math
\boxed{\text{Problem}\rightarrow\text{Gap}\rightarrow RQ\rightarrow\text{Objective}\rightarrow H}
```

## Evidence discipline

- factual background claims require qualified sources;
- gap claims must be traceable to Chapter 2 synthesis;
- `[H]` and `[P]` statements must not be written as established facts;
- proposal-stage results language is prohibited unless a verified pre-thesis/method result is explicitly identified.
