### Findings verifiable from authentic publisher/abstract alone
**Gong:** reports experimentally calibrated thermal transformer model (25 kVA), charging control and real load prediction; exact tested controller, differential equations, internal/external thermocouple locations, calibration/holdout conditions, EV service quality/real sensor traces **UNVERIFIED** absent paper. Earlier *Gong et al. (2012)* experimental test paper publicly has more content, but **is a separate study**, not 2015 original.

**Kunlasing:** document identity confirmed, ECTI-CON pages 400–403 from official proceedings TOC. The paper is about transformer load monitoring and EV charging control. **Do not assume actual transformer temperature sensor, heat-conditioned derating trigger, governing heat equations or independent validation from title alone**.

**Krama:** publisher abstract supports measured 50-kW air-cooled fast charger in ambient 15–55°C, physical rectifier temperature and offered/delivered DC power, electrical quality and SOC, thermal derating. Reports temporal/cross-session data-driven evaluation. This is direct **Tₑ component observed heat-response** evidence; no paired external P distribution transformer temperature or actual vehicle trip completion observed in publisher abstract. Raw data not released. **Original publisher equation coefficients are not independently authenticated; do not transplant from a Scribd transcription.**
