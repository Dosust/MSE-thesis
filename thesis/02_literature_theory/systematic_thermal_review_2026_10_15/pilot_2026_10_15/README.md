# Public-web pilot search — 2026-10-15

**Scope:** Six-stream preliminary search supporting proposed systematic-review protocol.

- [PILOT_SEARCH_REPORT.md](PILOT_SEARCH_REPORT.md) — executed search scope, source access, methodological risks, lane findings, and next gates.
- [PILOT_QUERY_LOG.csv](PILOT_QUERY_LOG.csv) — exact **executed public-web queries** and **blocked Elicit attempts**; no reliable per-query hit counts.
- [PILOT_CANDIDATE_RECORDS.csv](PILOT_CANDIDATE_RECORDS.csv) — 15 **NOT SCREENED** article/dataset leads with DOI, evidence class and unverified gaps.
- [PILOT_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md](PILOT_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md) — publisher/academic-record-supported preliminary claims with validation/data qualifications.
- [SENTINEL_SOURCE_CHECK.csv](SENTINEL_SOURCE_CHECK.csv) — source findability checks; **not** a formal query recall statistic.

**Do not conflate public-web pilot with Scopus, Web of Science, IEEE Xplore or Compendex searches.** Original PDFs have not been opened here; all candidate evidence is provisional. No accepted-core, canonical, PRISMA or method-selection status is changed by this pilot.
