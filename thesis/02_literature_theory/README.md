Determine what is established, contradictory, limited, unvalidated and unresolved before the thesis derives its framework. Chapter II must produce a qualified scientific boundary, not a bibliography accumulation.

## Evidence object

For each review question `Q`, construct

```math
\boxed{
\mathcal E_Q=(E_Q^+,E_Q^-,A_Q,M_Q,V_Q,U_Q,G_Q)
}
```

where:

- `E_Q^+`: supporting evidence;
- `E_Q^-`: contrary/rival evidence;
- `A_Q`: assumptions;
- `M_Q`: methods/models used by prior work;
- `V_Q`: validation evidence and domains;
- `U_Q`: uncertainty and limitations;
- `G_Q`: unresolved engineering question.

Each subsection must close with these elements in synthesized prose.

## Concept-definition synthesis

For every thesis-critical term, Chapter II must explicitly compare literature meanings before Chapter III adopts one definition:

```math
\boxed{
\text{literature definitions}
\rightarrow
\text{agreement/disagreement}
\rightarrow
\text{measurement/model implications}
\rightarrow
\text{adopted thesis definition in Chapter III}.
}
```

At minimum this applies to dependency, interdependency, interface, service, resilience, viability, reachability, effectiveness, efficiency, sustainability, criticality, uncertainty and transformation.

## Search and evidence discipline

Primary evidence enters through the active de novo search/FTEE/EGMIV protocols. Historical corpora may audit coverage but may not bypass current eligibility rules.

The review must preserve:

```math
\boxed{\text{supporting evidence}\neq\text{contrary evidence}\neq\text{novelty proof}.}
```

Field-level claims should use evidence appropriate to the claim. Definitions, equations, parameter values, specific mechanisms and empirical findings should be traced to primary/authoritative sources whenever feasible.

## Gap rule

A gap is not the absence of a paper in a selected bibliography. A thesis-relevant gap must identify:

```math
\boxed{
\text{established knowledge}
+\text{method/assumption boundary}
+\text{unresolved engineering consequence}
+\text{why the unresolved part matters to an RQ}.
}
```

Novelty remains a separate reproducible audit.

## Exit gate

Chapter II passes only if:

- declared search protocols were executed and preserved;
- screening/eligibility and exclusions are auditable;
- full-text evidence is qualified for the claims made;
- contrary/rival evidence is retained;
- definitions and assumptions are compared rather than silently merged;
- validation domains and limitations are extracted;
- adopted equations/parameters are source-traceable;
- the Chapter-I gap is supported by synthesis;
- no novelty claim is inferred from silence;
- references and in-text citations are verified.
