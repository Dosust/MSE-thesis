## Chair / GSC decisions still requiring explicit confirmation

| Decision | Current internal state | Required action |
|---|---|---|
| Citation/style guide | APA 7 currently used internally | chair/GSC confirmation before final manuscript freeze |
| Final chapter naming/order | seven-chapter architecture proposed | confirm with chair; ASU does not prescribe this exact chapter structure |
| Proposal page length/format | no verified universal 5–10 page MSE rule | follow chair/GSC instructions; do not label internal length as ASU requirement |
| Final thesis title | working/canonical research title may evolve | chair/GSC approval before title-page freeze |
| Exact defense semester/deadlines | not frozen here | retrieve official semester calendar when defense term is known |
