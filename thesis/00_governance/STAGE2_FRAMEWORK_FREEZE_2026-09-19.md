## 3. Frozen items

## 8. Stage-2 closure rule

Stage 2 remains ACTIVE until, for the intended thesis case applications:

1. every selected Stage-1 P–T candidate has a final Stage-2 status;
2. every candidate status trajectory is retained;
3. every PASS relation has explicit evidence provenance, validity and falsification;
4. every BLOCKED relation records exact missing evidence;
5. every FAIL relation retains contradiction/rejection evidence;
6. case-invariance of the rules has been tested across intended applications;
7. every accepted relation has a Stage-2→3 readiness decision;
8. RQ2 is answered from the resulting evidence-grounded causal sets.

Only then may a separate governance action declare:

\`\`\`text
STAGE 2 CLOSED / FROZEN
\`\`\`

and formally activate Stage 3 as the current thesis stage.
