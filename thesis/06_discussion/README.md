## Purpose

Interpret validated results against theory, rival explanations, infrastructure practice and sustainable-engineering objectives without exceeding the declared evidence or validation domain.

## Required questions

```math
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&\text{What mechanism was demonstrated or challenged?}\\
&\text{Under what conditions?}\\
&\text{With what uncertainty?}\\
&\text{What rival explanation remains?}\\
&\text{Which assumptions materially affect the inference?}\\
&\text{What engineering decision changes?}\\
&\text{What remains unvalidated?}
\end{aligned}}
```

## Assumption-dependence rule

For every conclusion materially dependent on assumption `A_i`, state:

```math
\boxed{
A_i
\rightarrow
\text{result sensitivity}
\rightarrow
\text{whether the engineering conclusion changes}.
}
```

A limitation should identify the mechanism by which it could bias, weaken or restrict an inference rather than merely naming a generic issue such as “uncertainty.”

## Intervention interpretation rule

Preserve:

```math
\boxed{\text{effectiveness}\neq\text{efficiency}\neq\text{sustainability}}.
```

A resilience-effective intervention is not automatically resource-efficient or sustainable. Sustainability implications must be tied to measured/modelled outcomes and declared accounting boundaries rather than generic policy claims.

## Interface/pathway rule

Interface criticality is discussed as a supporting diagnostic unless a separate superiority test was preregistered. Adaptive/sequential transformation is discussed as a supporting extension unless separately activated and validated.

## Generalization discipline

Generalization cannot exceed the validation domain. Distinguish:

- conclusions supported in the demonstrator/test domain;
- transfer hypotheses requiring additional validation;
- future-work extensions to additional sectors, hazards, geographies or organizational regimes.

## Exit gate

Every interpretation must trace to registered results plus qualified evidence, address relevant uncertainty/rival explanations, and state its validity domain. See `../00_governance/THESIS_COMPLETION_GATES.md`.
