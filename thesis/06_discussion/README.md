# Chapter 6 — Discussion and Sustainable Engineering Implications

## Purpose

Interpret the validated results against theory, rival explanations, infrastructure practice and sustainable-engineering objectives.

## Required questions

```math
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&\text{What mechanism was demonstrated?}\\
&\text{Under what conditions?}\\
&\text{With what uncertainty?}\\
&\text{What engineering decision changes?}\\
&\text{What remains unvalidated?}
\end{aligned}}
```

## Recommended sections

1. summary of H1–H5 outcomes;
2. mechanistic interpretation;
3. comparison with prior literature;
4. implications for infrastructure-system representation;
5. implications for resilience engineering;
6. sustainability implications;
7. engineering decision implications;
8. transferability/generalization limits;
9. limitations;
10. unresolved uncertainty and research needs.

## Discipline

Sustainability implications must be connected to measurable or modeled system behavior rather than generic policy claims. Generalization cannot exceed the declared validation domain.

## Exit gate

Every interpretation must trace to registered results and qualified evidence. See `../00_governance/THESIS_COMPLETION_GATES.md`.
