## Interface-criticality boundary

Interface criticality is a supporting diagnostic unless a separate comparative superiority test is preregistered. The framework may define and use the diagnostic without implying that it is H4.

## Transformation-pathway boundary

Sequential/adaptive transformation pathways are conditional Stage-7 extensions. They enter the primary thesis only if their action/state-transition semantics, feasibility/resource constraints, uncertainty treatment, comparator and decision rule are separately defined and testable.

## Epistemic rule

Every construct is labeled during development as `[E]`, `[D]`, `[H]`, `[P]`, `[A]`, `[U]` or `[R]` under the thesis writing standard.

```math
\boxed{\text{mathematical sophistication}\neq\text{scientific establishment}.}
```

An equation cannot promote a proposal into an established theory or a hypothesis into a result.

## Mathematical-object rule

For every important object declare, where applicable:

- type;
- domain/codomain;
- units/dimensions;
- indices;
- physical meaning;
- evidence/derivation status;
- assumptions;
- uncertainty;
- validity domain;
- downstream role.

## Figure rule

High-level framework figures appear only after the underlying objects and handoffs are defined. Generated visuals remain drafts until checked against the canonical mathematics and visual ontology.

A communication simplification may remove labels but may not alter scientific meaning.

## Exit gate

Chapter III passes only if:

- all symbols and critical concepts are defined;
- units/domains are declared where applicable;
- every causal arrow has a mechanism and admissibility logic;
- established/adopted mathematics is source-traceable;
- thesis-derived and proposed constructs are visibly separated from established theory;
- every equation is dimensionally/logically consistent;
- constituent physics remain admissible;
- Stage-5 viability/reachability/probability semantics are preserved;
- effectiveness, efficiency and sustainability are distinct;
- interface criticality and adaptive transformation are not overpromoted;
- stage-to-stage handoffs are explicit;
- framework figures exactly match the mathematics.
