## Governance / proposal contract

- [ ] Cover/title exactly matches the V3 official title (including en dash) and has been checked against any ASU/chair administrative requirements.
- [ ] Frozen General Objective and SO1–SO3 are reproduced **verbatim**, with no fourth independent objective.
- [ ] Three **working** research questions are clearly marked **PROPOSED / NOT FROZEN**, and their SO1–SO3 alignment has chair review.
- [ ] No September RQ1–RQ7/SO1–SO7/H1–H5/seven-stage structures are presented as active thesis claims or unapproved hypotheses.
- [ ] ASU/SSEBE/chair proposal formatting and length requirements are verified rather than invented.

## Physics and system boundary

- [ ] Power System P and full Electrified Transportation System Tₑ are physically distinguished.
- [ ] EVSE, charging connection and relevant power-transfer control belong to Tₑ, **not** a third system; actual scope vs active model selection distinguished.
- [ ] Study is **thermal interdependencies only** under extreme heat; common ambient exposure does not imply directional coupling.
- [ ] Each P→Tₑ or Tₑ→P pathway independently identifies equipment, observed state, heat constraint, carried variable and receiving-side measurable effect.
- [ ] Thermal state, capability, functionality, service and resilience remain distinct; SOC is not mislabeled thermal state.
- [ ] All actual operating limits, equipment ratings and thermal thresholds have compatible source provenance, units and applicability.

## Literature / novelty

- [ ] Formal Scopus, WoS, IEEE Xplore and Compendex database searches executed with exact syntax, date, query filters, native exports, deduplication and proper PRISMA counts.
- [ ] Primary original paper equations, figures, validation targets, raw-data claims and governing assumptions are inspected to a declared tier.
- [ ] Prior art Godina, Blomgren, Bragone, EV-EcoSim, Villarroel and Krama addressed with source-accurate claim boundaries.
- [ ] Proposed gap is defensible after formal review; no assertion of novelty by mere combination of EV charging, transformer thermal aging or co-simulation.
- [ ] APA 7 author lists and source metadata checked, with in-text citations alongside each externally sourced scientific claim.

## Data and mathematical method

- [ ] Actual original timestamped measurement records opened lawfully with equipment IDs, units, sensor placement, quality flags and timestamps verified.
- [ ] Heat forcing, load, charger operation and relevant control confounders characterized at sufficient resolutions and coverage.
- [ ] Training vs independent heat event/session/site test partitions fixed prior to model evaluation; sampling/leakage controls reported.
- [ ] Equations are tagged governing/literature/standard/thesis-derived and verified dimensionally with initial/boundary conditions.
- [ ] Identifiability, observability, parameter sensitivity and physical consistency tested; failure recorded without hidden parameter substitution.
- [ ] PHY, GREY and PIML (only where justified) evaluated against identical observed targets and uncertainty protocols; method not preselected.
- [ ] Matched cross-system field measurement **or** explicit simulation-only claim boundary established; unrelated component records not labeled observed coupling.

## SO3 service and scientific inference

- [ ] Critical-time events tied to sourced constraints and mathematically precise crossing definitions.
- [ ] Intra-equipment thermal lag separately reported from **directionally attributable** P–Tₑ propagation time.
- [ ] Real electricity-service requirement and measured outcome defined, or service claim marked NOT ESTABLISHED.
- [ ] Mobility service requirement (e.g., departure needed energy/actual trip) observed or justified, with charging-duration surrogate labeled correctly.
- [ ] Independent performance metrics, residual/physical-law checks, uncertainty intervals and falsification controls recorded.
- [ ] Resilience results based on service trajectories, not merely physical component survival; recovery only if modeled.
- [ ] Optional two-pathway sustainability indicators sourced and bounded, not universal SDG performance.

## External approvals and release

- [ ] Advisor/chair confirms feasibility, preferred bounded application, engineering deliverables and proposal acceptance path.
- [ ] Data rights, consent/access and any confidentiality/ethics restrictions resolved.
- [ ] Final proposal contains a source-quality and limitations statement, and no unsupported field validation or novelty claims.
- [ ] A final proposal-specific change log and approval record exists.

**Decision:** `NOT RELEASE-READY` until the required scientific and administrative gates have independently passed. The existence of a coherent draft is not an approval or a completed experiment.
