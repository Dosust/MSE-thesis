# Authenticity and Recall Policy

## Verbatim
Only text directly available from a visible user message or attached source and copied without rewriting may be labeled `VERBATIM`.

## Reconstructed scientific state
A structured summary of equations, conclusions, open decisions, literature guardrails and artifact locations may be labeled `RECONSTRUCTED_SCIENTIFIC_STATE`. It must not be presented as a byte-for-byte assistant transcript.

## Hidden/internal content
System/developer messages, hidden chain-of-thought and internal tool payloads are not part of the user-visible archive and must not be reconstructed as if they were.

## Literature provenance labels
Use one of:
- `SUPPORTED_IN_207`
- `SUPPORTED_BY_ATTACHED_SOURCE`
- `EXTERNAL_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED`
- `THESIS_INTEGRATING`
- `CANDIDATE_CONTRIBUTION`

## Decision provenance labels
Use one of:
- `USER_FROZEN`
- `WORKING_ASSUMPTION`
- `ASSISTANT_RECOMMENDATION`
- `OPEN_DECISION`
- `SUPERSEDED`

Current Type I/II/III selection: `OPEN_DECISION`.
